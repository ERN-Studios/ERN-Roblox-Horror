### Bug fixes

1. **Two spectate cameras fought after death.** RoundUI's `CameraType.Custom`
   ticker is deleted; SpectateController is the only camera writer and now stops
   itself when `RoundActive` goes false (guarded on its own `spectating` flag so
   JumpscareUI's kill cam is left alone). The never-shown SpectateBanner is gone.
2. **Gamepad could neither sprint nor use the flashlight.** L2 (hold) sprints,
   R1 toggles the light, both with UIDevice captions. `sprintRequested()` in
   NoiseReporter is the single definition of "asking to sprint", with a
   hardware re-read so a stuck trigger cannot pin the lobby to sprint speed.
3. **Emergency Re-entry spent the credit after the respawn.** `useReentry` now
   reserves, invokes, and refunds on refusal (token-keyed, three retries, a loud
   warn if the refund never lands). A purchase made while dead in a live round
   auto-uses itself. `docs/ZYNTRA_MONETIZATION_SETUP.md` updated; the 15 s window
   length is the one thing still standing between the product and on-sale.
4. **Pool Foam never marked whom it hunted.** `Level2_PoolFoamTargeted` and
   `BeingChased` are now reference-counted across the five entities and cleared
   on death, target drop and `Controller.Stop`. NoiseReporter's adrenaline and
   EntityShakeController were waiting on exactly this.
5. **Level 1 announced only the first escape.** PuzzleManager fires the shared
   `RoundStatus` "escape" per escape, per in-round recipient, with the name;
   RoundUI shows "<Name> found a way out" and replaces rather than stacks.

### Features

- **Pool Foam hears the NoiseRegistry** (`Level 2 Pool Foam Configuration.Hearing`).
  Pumps announce themselves every 2 s while the motor runs; the controller owns
  the `ReportNoise` intake for Level 2 (EntityAI is disabled there). Hearing
  steers target choice and patrol; the look-latch and speed ramp are untouched.
- **Two players per Level 3 table** (`Hiding.HideOccupantCap = 2`, lanes at
  ±2 studs). The prompt stays enabled at one occupant; hidden players have
  `ProximityPromptService` disabled so E cannot re-fire a prompt.
- **The Mall Manager checks tables** (`Level 3 Configuration.TableCheck`):
  sweep bias toward occupied tables, `TABLE_CHECK` state with a 2 s warning
  ("SOMETHING IS LOOKING UNDER THE TABLE" on the occupants' hide banner and the
  blackout vocal at the table), then a flush to the far side with 1.5 s attack
  immunity, 25 s per-table and 18 s global cooldowns, plus a mid-hunt detour only
  toward a table closer than the nearest exposed player.
- **PARTY DOWN card** for the 15 s wipe window: who fell last, bar and seconds,
  the re-entry action (credit or 29 R$ product 3707755318) and NO THANKS, both
  inert for the first 0.6 s, gamepad selection landing on NO THANKS. Contract:
  `"partydown", 15, name` / `"partydownclear"` on `RoundStatus`.
- **Badges** on the level-completed hook: `ZyntraConfig.Badges` with four keys at
  0 (disabled) until the owner creates them; profile gains `LevelsCleared` and
  `AwardedBadges`; awards are idempotent and non-fatal. Trello card "Opret 4
  Roblox-badges" holds the icon spec and where the ids go.
