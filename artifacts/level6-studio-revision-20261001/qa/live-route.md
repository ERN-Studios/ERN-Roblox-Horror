# Level 6 real-UI route for the current Play session

Use the running Studio client for every E/B/R action. The nearby Luau probes only observe live instances and replicated attributes; they never call a runtime ModuleScript from `execute_luau` (which has an isolated require cache). Coordinates are seed-specific, so run [`server_route_probe.luau`](server_route_probe.luau) in the Play **server** after every milestone. Its distances are straight-line walking hints, not proof of an open path. Run [`client_snapshot.luau`](client_snapshot.luau) and [`client_lighting_probe.luau`](client_lighting_probe.luau) in the Play **client**.

1. **Find and carry all five.** CD 01 is already verified carried in the first Play. Press **R** to show the top-right CD reader, which points to the nearest still-pickable disc; press **B** to show the five developer ESP highlights, including the carried visual. For each remaining WORLD or DROPPED index in `server_route_probe.cds`, walk to its `CollectPrompt` (within 9 studs and line of sight) and hold **E** for 0.35 s. WORLD prompt: `COLLECT CD` / `PARTY MIX CD nn`; DROPPED prompt: `PICK UP DROPPED CD` / `BIRTHDAY MUSIC CD nn`. After each pickup, verify that exact `Level6_CDnnState=CARRIED`, `Level6_CDCollectedProgress` increments, and the player's `Level6_HeldCDCount`/mask changes. Check that B still highlights the carried model and R no longer targets the collected disc. The VCR accepts all carried CDs in one interaction, so carrying CD 01..05 together is valid; deposit sooner if survival requires it.
2. **Hide and leave.** Walk to one of `server_route_probe.nearestHide` and hold **E** at `HIDE UNDER TABLE` / `FOLDING TABLE nn`. Verify player `Level6_Hiding=true`, `Level6_HideTableIndex>0`, state `Level6_HiddenPlayers=1`, hide camera/pose and the on-screen `Level6TableHideUI.LeaveHiding` button. Click `LEAVE HIDING` or press **E**; verify hiding, occupied table, and hidden count reset. Gamepad B/X also exits hiding. This must be an actual prompt and button/key input.
3. **Blackout and Manager, before the last insert.** With at least one CD picked up and the exit still locked, record `client_lighting_probe` at normal light. To bound waiting, the root may invoke the existing Studio-only `ServerStorage.Level6DevSkipToPreBlackout:Invoke()` **once** in the Play server; it should return `true, SKIPPED_TO_2_25` and counts as **dev-seek coverage**. Watch replicated `Level6_RoomSongPhase`: `PRE_BLACKOUT` near 145 s, `BLACKOUT_SONG` at 150 s, `BLACKOUT_HUNT` after the ~180 s song end, then `RECOVERY_FLICKER` around 210 s. Capture the client probe during blackout and recovery: nearest fluorescent carrier **and its Blender MeshPart children** must darken/restore together, as must the authored gateway `__glow` chunks; enabled-light count and viewport should agree. During hunt, use `server_route_probe.manager` (`State`, `HuntActive`, `PathStatus`, `PathValidated`, `TargetMode/Distance`, `SpawnSerial`) and watch the Manager traverse a kitchen/utility/staff gateway's two portals without repeated path failures or a stall. A source-only pass is insufficient. Completing five inserts holds the final route dark, so test recovery first.
4. **Insert and escape.** Walk to `server_route_probe.vcr` and use its actual **E** `InsertCDPrompt` (0.55 s, within 9 studs and line of sight), labeled `INSERT CARRIED CDS` / `BIRTHDAY CD PLAYER n/5`. One interaction inserts every held CD. If deposited in batches, repeat until five unique `Level6_CDnnState=INSERTED`, `Level6_CDInsertedProgress=5`, `Level6_ExitUnlocked=true`, and `Level 6 Energon Freight Exit.Level6_ExitPowered=true`. The concealed portal opens into the **lightless** final hall; `Level6_ExitGuideLampCount=0` is expected. Walk through the portal and run into the freight doorway at `server_route_probe.escape` (`EscapeTrigger`). A real trigger crossing briefly sets `Level6Escaped=true`, then Preview Runtime automatically returns the player to the lobby and clears it. Do not set that attribute or call `Runtime.Leave` to substitute for the escape.
5. **Cleanup and seeded rejoin.** If aborting before escape, use **E** (0.5 s) on `Level6DeveloperPreviewReturnPrompt`, `RETURN TO LOBBY`, at the original `Level6Exit` preview marker near entry. Once the last participant leaves, run [`server_snapshot.luau`](server_snapshot.luau) and `client_snapshot`: generated world absent, `Level6InRound`/`Level6PreviewActive` cleared, objective/hide/music/Manager state reset, no leftover ESP highlights or active lights/sounds/entity. At the lobby door, use **E** on `ENTER PARTY BACKROOMS` for a fresh real rejoin. Compare `Level6_Generation`, resolved seed and layout hash with the prior run; `Level6_GeneratedRoomCount=32` and all three gateway families `RoomKitchenPrep`, `RoomUtilityHall`, `RoomStaffNook` should be present. Verify a second CD pickup and return cleanup. Different random seeds require distinct observed seed values, not an assumption.
6. **Bounded performance.** During an active round, run [`server_perf_10s.luau`](server_perf_10s.luau) in the Play server and [`client_perf_10s.luau`](client_perf_10s.luau) in the Play client. Repeat during Manager hunt only if that phase has a distinct concern. Record p50/p95 scheduler/physics times, local FPS/render time, memory, and visible stalls. These ten-second solo samples do not establish multiplayer capacity or published-server behavior.
