# Object and consumable icon review

Reviewed the eight current catalog images downloaded from the fresh Studio catalog, at their original size and with the intended 52 px shop use in mind.

| Catalog key | Current problem | Replacement subject | Benefit accuracy constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EntityDetector | Four screws, manufactured metal, glow and corridor backdrop dominate the small thumbnail. | Simple handheld detector with three threat bars. | Avoid radar dots or a map: the item reports LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH, never exact entity positions. |
| Supporter | Ornate teal star medal looks like a generic combat achievement. | Simple printed supporter ID/tag. | This grants a supporter tag and 10 tokens once, not a gameplay tool. |
| AdvancedEquipment | Helmet and colored arc primarily sell an outdated equipment fantasy. | Focused flashlight with a narrow beam. | Current primary feature is a focused torch with greater range; do not imply a protective helmet. |
| CosmeticEquipment | Three glowsticks are a valid subject, but bloom, arcs and reflections clutter them. | Three clean, colored glowstick silhouettes. | Permanent color customization is cosmetic; avoid shield or enhanced power cues. |
| ExpeditionPack | Ornate equipment case and cylinders resemble a futuristic lootbox. | Grouped re-entry, shield and three route-marker symbols. | Exactly one re-entry, one shield charge and three markers; no random rewards or permanent equipment. |
| Tokens4 | Glossy square objects lack a clear denomination and look like futuristic processors. | Shared research-token motif with a large 4. | An immediate grant of four tokens, distinct from an earning multiplier. |
| Tokens20 | Five stacks are dense and the grant quantity cannot be read immediately. | Same research-token motif with a large 20. | An immediate grant of twenty tokens, not five stacks, a multiplier, or infinite currency. |
| EmergencyReentry | Portal frame, gold detailing and red glow obscure the usable return symbol. | Bold doorway and return arrow. | Return after death; no healing, extra life count, or guaranteed-safe-location claim. |

Common direction pending the root's shared design reference and Claude critique: matte industrial research-terminal pictograms, ivory/charcoal, muted teal/amber, broad silhouettes, generous negative space, no ornate rims, glossy chrome, bloom or background scene. The generated images will carry semantic symbols; normal UI keeps the exact names, prices and descriptions.
