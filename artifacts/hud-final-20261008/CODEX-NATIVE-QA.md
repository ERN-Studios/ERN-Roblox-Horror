## Input classifier and coverage limits

UIDevice determines form factor from an explicit ForceTouch override when present; otherwise it requires TouchEnabled and the absence of the combined MouseEnabled+KeyboardEnabled capability pair (`UIDevice.ModuleScript.lua:162`). LastInputType does not determine touch form factor; its event only refreshes desktop/gamepad hint selection (`:1999`). Pointer/VirtualKeyboard activity alone is therefore not a causal explanation for a template change.

At 666x374, raw TouchEnabled can coexist with both mouse and keyboard capabilities, yielding the hybrid desktop descriptor without an override. The clean lobby report states `touch=false`; the Level3 warning probe renders PC E. The live reports state `touch=true`, while a later card snapshot states Touch=false. Those observations are reported as observed capability/override states, without attributing them to last input. Final Level1 verification pins ForceTouchUI=true. The probes establish native touch-descriptor geometry and physical mouse click delivery; they do not prove physical touch-only hardware.
