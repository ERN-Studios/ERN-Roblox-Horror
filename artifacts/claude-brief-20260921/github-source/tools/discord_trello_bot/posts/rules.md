# Welcome to BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET

Co-op horror on Roblox. Grab your friends, stay quiet, and find the way out. Something is listening.

This server is where we build the game together with you: bug reports, ideas, clips and finding people to play with.

# Rules

1. **Be decent.** No harassment, slurs, or drama.
2. **No spam.** No self-promotion, unrelated links, or pinging everyone.
3. **No cheating.** No exploits, and no sharing other people's personal info.
4. **Right channel.** Bugs go in 🐛-bugs, ideas in 💡-feedback, everything else in 💬-general.
5. **Team's word is final.** Disagree politely, in DMs.

# How to report a bug

1. Open 🐛-bugs and click **New Post**.
2. **Title:** what happens. **Text:** how to make it happen, which level, and a screenshot or video if you have one.
3. Pick a tag. One bug per post.

👀 on your post means it is on the team's board.
✅ means it is fixed and live in the game.

# Ideas and feedback

Same thing in 💡-feedback, one idea per post. ✅ means it made it into the game.

# Roles

**Team** builds and moderates. **Supporter** owns the Zyntra Supporter pass. **Tester** helps test new levels before release.
