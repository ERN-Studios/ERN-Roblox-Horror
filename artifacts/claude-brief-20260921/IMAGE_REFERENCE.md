## Prompt brugt til generering

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: internal environment art-direction reference for Roblox game BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET, Level 4, THE QUIET SUBURBS — Zyntra Residential Test Site. Create one polished landscape concept board, 1536x1024 or comparable landscape resolution. It is a design reference, NOT an advertisement or a claim of implemented game content. Main composition (about 75%): eye-height wide view of a deceptively peaceful, empty, liminal suburban test neighbourhood in warm still afternoon light, clear washed blue sky, unnaturally uniform green lawns, 8–12 modest nearly identical detached houses along a walkable loop road with a clearly visible pedestrian shortcut. Houses use faded cream, dusty yellow, and blue-grey plaster or simple siding, dark roofs, precisely trimmed hedges, modest fences and mailboxes. An isolated water/signal tower is the navigation landmark in the distance; a small empty bus shelter and restrained graphite Zyntra signal cabinet with a tiny mint status light mark the exit. Include subtle readable anomalies: one extra window, one reversed mailbox, asymmetrical curtains, no chaotic clutter. Use believable simplified modular game geometry and restrained surface detail suitable for mobile Roblox, not a photoreal AAA city or blocky toy aesthetic. Lower three narrow reference panels: one repeatable empty house interior with an ordinary sofa, blank picture frame and CRT television; one close view of a porch's readable green safety signal with a muted amber warning alternative; one neutral full-body silhouette study of the original entity THE NEIGHBOUR, very tall and slender in stiff faded maintenance workwear, too-long forearms, matte opaque glass face mask, slight unnatural head tilt, slow purposeful posture. No Slenderman suit/tie, no copied monster, no gore. Keep palette and materials coherent across panels, soft atmospheric light and readable shadows. Header text only 'LEVEL 4 / THE QUIET SUBURBS'; small subtitle 'ENVIRONMENT CONCEPT — NOT IN-GAME'; panel labels only 'MODULAR INTERIOR', 'READABLE SAFETY SIGNAL', 'THE NEIGHBOUR'. Crisp restrained typography, no invented gameplay HUD, no decorative neon everywhere, no logos other than small ZYNTRA on the cabinet. Match the supplied textual design brief precisely. Return a local file path for project use.
