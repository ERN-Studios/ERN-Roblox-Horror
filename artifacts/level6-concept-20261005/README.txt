LEVEL 6 - NEW CONCEPT (DRAFT), 2026-10-05

Nothing in the game has changed. These are pictures of a proposal, to say yes or no to.


WHAT THE DRAFT DOES

  Hall            600 x 400 studs  ->  864 x 576 studs (2.1 times the floor)
  Roof            46 studs         ->  120 studs
  The Big Frame   10% of the floor ->  47% of the floor (9.8 times the footprint)
  Floors in it    3                ->  6 everywhere, 7 in Tube Town, 10 in the Tower
  Longest slide   20 studs of drop ->  100 studs

  - The Big Frame (the soft-play structure of posts, nets, decks, tubes and slides) is the map now.
    It has five parts: Net Maze, Tube Town, Foam Forest, the Tower, and the Ball Ocean inside it.
  - Home Base moves into the middle of the frame: a round court with the frame on every side.
    Whichever way you run from the post, you run into the nets.
  - Three gates lead in (west, south, east). The lanes inside turn before they get long, so the
    Counter never gets a long straight view.
  - Arcade and Prizes are removed. Tube Town stands against that wall.
  - Reception, Snack Shack, Party Rooms, Toddler Town, Inflatables and Staff Only (with the exit)
    stay, round the edge, about the size they are now.
  - The ball pit stays almost empty.

WHICH PICTURES TO TRUST

  02, 03 and 04 are drawn from the numbers above. Every size in them is exact.
  The rest are generated pictures. They show the scale, the height and the mood the draft is after.
  They are not exact: the number of floors is not always six, a few tubes change colour along their
  length (in the game each slide stays one colour), the Ball Ocean has a few too many balls in one
  corner, and the layout in 06 and 15 only roughly follows the plan. The game will not look as
  photographic as 06 to 16; picture 05 is the closest to how it would look in Roblox.


WHAT A BUILD WOULD REMOVE (needs your yes first)

  - the arcade cabinets, the claw machines, the air hockey table and the arcade carpet
  - the prize shelf with its toys, and the Arcade and Prizes signs
  The party button of the easter egg sits on the arcade carpet today. It would need a new place.


THREE THINGS TO DECIDE BEFORE BUILDING

  1. Does the Counter climb? With six to ten floors, a player on an upper floor is out of its reach
     unless it can go up. The draft assumes wide soft ramps on the lanes so it reaches every floor,
     while tubes and crawl holes stay too small for it.
  2. The count. The hall is twice as big and the count is about 20 seconds. From Home Base the
     frame is right there, so it may be enough; it has to be tried.
  3. The build. The frame would be about twenty times as many cells as today. As loose parts that
     is too heavy for phones, so it would be built in Blender as joined meshes, not part by part.
