Image `04_the_gate_between_the_nets` (references: refs/style_frame.png, refs/sheet_ingame.jpg)
Standing three metres in front of the West Gate of the frame: a gateway 4 metres wide between two tall black
net fences on padded yellow and red posts, a padded blue lintel above it carrying a worn painted sign that
reads exactly "PLAY ZONE". Beyond the gate a lane of checker floor runs ten metres into the frame and turns out
of sight; decks, nets and tubes stacked six storeys overhead on both sides close over the lane like a canyon.
Only one working fluorescent tube far above; the lane is darker than the hall. Looking slightly upward so the
height shows.

Image `05_inside_the_net_maze` (references: refs/style_frame.png, refs/sheet_ingame.jpg)
Inside the ground floor of the frame: a junction of narrow corridors, each about 1.5 metres wide, walled with
black safety netting between padded posts, the mat deck of the next storey low overhead (2.7 m), three ways to
choose and each one bends away after a few metres. Through the layers of netting you can make out more
corridors, a padded crawl hole, a short flight of soft foam steps going up, hanging punch bags. Light falls in
thin shafts through the decks above. Claustrophobic, a place to hide. Camera at child eye height.
