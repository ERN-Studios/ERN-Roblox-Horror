LEVEL 6 - THE ARENA, DRAFT 3, 2026-10-06

Nothing in the game has changed. These are pictures of a proposal, to say yes or no to.
This replaces the draft with the stepped rings.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST DRAFT

  - The structure round the post is no longer a staircase of rings. It is a wall of galleries: a blend
    of the two pictures you picked (the gallery looking down on the court, and the twelve floors
    standing straight up round it).
  - The way out is decided: the third touch starts a one-minute countdown on the post, the post goes
    down into the floor, and the exit is under it.


WHAT IT IS

  One structure, wall to wall. Nothing else.

  Hall            600 x 400 studs  ->  round, 548 studs across
  Roof            46 studs         ->  150 studs
  The frame       10% of the floor ->  95% of the floor
  Floors          3                ->  12, from the court to the wall

  - THE POST stands in a round court in the exact middle, 88 studs across. The Counter stands at it.
  - THE WALL OF GALLERIES. Round the court the frame goes four floors straight up, steps back one
    narrow ledge (16 studs), four more floors, another narrow ledge, four more: twelve floors, 120
    studs, leaning back only slightly. Behind that face the frame is solid, twelve floors deep, all
    the way to the hall wall.
  - WHAT SEES THE POST. Every gallery on floors 1 to 4, and the top two floors above each ledge
    (7, 8, 11 and 12). From floors 5, 6, 9 and 10 the ledge below hides the foot of the post, and you
    see the far side of the court instead. Two net bridges cross right above it.
  - THE COUNTER'S WAYS. Two ring corridors and the short corridors between them are wide enough for
    it on every floor, with wide stairs where they meet, so no floor is out of its reach. Tubes,
    crawl holes and everything between the corridors stay too small for it.
  - YOU ARRIVE in a small tunnel through the wall, right in front of the PLAY ZONE gate. Through the
    gate one straight lane, 228 studs, runs through the structure to the court (14 seconds at a
    walk). The intro starts when you pass the gate.


HOW THE ROUND ENDS

  1  THE THIRD TOUCH   Every light turns red, as today. The post starts counting down from 60.
  2  THE MINUTE        Digits glow round the top of the post, and the yellow ring on the floor is a
                       clock of sixty segments that go dark one by one, so it can be read from
                       every gallery that sees the post. The Counter hunts. Stay alive.
  3  ZERO              The post sinks into the floor. Green light comes up out of the hole.
  4  DOWN              A slide winds down the shaft round the post to a small room under the court.
  5  OUT               The green EXIT door. Through it the level is cleared.

  The tunnel you arrive in and the room under the court are both too low for the Counter (it is 8.2
  studs tall). Once you are on the slide it cannot follow.

  Things I chose that you did not specify, all easy to change:
  - The clock is on the floor as well as on the post, so players high up can read it.
  - The hole stays open until everyone still standing is out or caught, as the exit does today.
  - The post goes down the middle of the shaft and the slide goes round it, so the post never blocks
    the hole.

TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE A BUILD

  1. Size. This is about 36 times as much frame as today (504 cells -> about 18 400), a third more
     than the stepped draft, because the full twelve floors now run all the way in to the court. As
     loose parts that is impossible, so it would be built in Blender as one slice of joined meshes
     repeated eight times round the post. I would build one slice first and measure it on a phone
     before the rest. If it is too heavy, the hall gets narrower, not lower.
  2. Sight. Today the Counter kills what it sees. Standing at the post it would look straight into
     every gallery that looks at it, so its sight needs a rule (how far, and whether netting hides
     you) before the round is fun.
