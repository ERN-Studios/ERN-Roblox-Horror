## What changes against round one (everything else stays EXACTLY the same mall: same ceiling, floor, wall stripe,
## columns, light, emptiness; pass a round-one picture as a reference every time so it is the same place)
- SHOPS OPEN. Each of the six level shops is a wide open shopfront, as wide as the unit, with the shutter rolled all
  the way up into its box (only the box shows) and a plain backlit sign box above. Inside, the unit is dressed as a
  shop-window SHOWCASE of its level: the back two thirds are a small stage set of that level's real look, softly lit
  from inside, clearly visible from the concourse. In the front third, on the shop's own floor, is the QUEUE BAY as
  in the present lobby (see `refs/today_lobby_e.jpg`): THREE round low pads in a row, each about two players wide, a
  dark green disc with a paler ring, each with a small upright sign post beside it reading STATION 1, STATION 2,
  STATION 3. Players stand on a pad to start the level.
- THE ZYNTRA STAND. In the middle of the concourse, where the fountain was (the fountain is gone), a small
  freestanding mall kiosk, the kind that sells phone cases: a square island of waist-high glass display counters on
  all four sides with a gap to walk in, a low canopy with a backlit sign: ZYNTRA. In the lit glass cases and on short
  racks: the game's things (see the product posters on the shop wall in `refs/today_lobby_a.jpg` and
  `refs/today_lobby_b.jpg`): small boxed gadgets, a shield device, small potion bottles, packs of route markers,
  torches and batteries, and a short rail of hooded hazmat suits in yellow, pale blue, cream, black-and-gold and
  purple. Nobody behind the counter. It is the brightest thing in the mall.
- THE END. No fence. Where the lit concourse ends, a line of waist-high poles (black-and-yellow striped posts on
  round bases) carries yellow crime-scene tape across the whole width, sagging a little, printed: CRIME SCENE DO NOT
  CROSS. A person can duck under it. Past it the SAME concourse goes on with every light dead: the same columns and
  shops, black inside, fading to total darkness.
- THE THING IN THE DARK (one picture only). It is never seen as a body. Far down the dark wing two large round
  eyes glow dull amber, level with a ceiling; from the blackness under them four very long, thin, pale, clammy arms
  with big hands lie stretched along the terrazzo toward the camera, the nearest hand about ten steps away, fingers
  spread on the floor. Nothing else: no face, no teeth, no torso, no blood.
