## THE LOOK, which is what round two got wrong: 1990s, and BACKROOMS
Round two reads as a new mall: glossy polished floor with reflections, black fascias with crisp white modern lettering,
LED-bright cool light, sleek glass. Wrong. This is a tired provincial shopping mall fitted out about 1989 to 1994 and
never updated, seen the way the Backrooms are seen: everything a little yellow, a little dim, a little damp, flat and
humming. Make every picture obey this:
- LIGHT: old fluorescent tube troffers behind yellowed prismatic plastic lenses, giving a flat, warm, slightly
  green-yellow light; the whole picture has that mono-yellow backrooms cast. Some tubes dead, one dim. No LED strips,
  no spotlights, no neon outside the cinema's showcase, no bright white.
- CEILING: low suspended ceiling of 2x2 acoustic tiles, off-white gone cream, some stained brown, a few sagging.
- FLOOR: MATTE. Worn commercial vinyl tile or dull beige-and-brown terrazzo with a brown border, no reflections, no
  shine; or low-pile patterned carpet in muted mustard and brown near the shops.
- WALLS: yellowed cream textured plaster or faded mustard-yellow wallpaper with a faint damask pattern (the Backrooms
  wallpaper), a brown ceramic tile skirting, and the faded teal-and-mauve stripe at hand height from round one.
- TRIM AND FITTINGS: oak-effect wood laminate, brass-coloured rails and edging gone dull, smoked or bronze glass,
  beige plastic. Fake plants in brown plastic tubs. A coin payphone, a gumball machine, a CRT television on a wall
  bracket are the kind of thing that belongs; do not overfill.
- SIGNS: backlit plastic sign boxes, cream or pale yellow faces gone uneven, with chunky 1990s lettering: bold rounded
  or bold italic capitals in dark red, brown or teal. NOT thin modern sans-serif, NOT white letters on black.
- RENDERING: as in round one's brief ("Rendering"): a screenshot of a Roblox horror game, plain buildable geometry,
  matte flat materials, a wide game-camera lens at standing eye height. No film grain, haze, god rays, lens flare.
  No people, no players. Empty, still, lit as if someone will come back.
- Only the signs named in a picture carry letters, spelled EXACTLY as written here, capitals as written.
