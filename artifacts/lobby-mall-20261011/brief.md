## The place (the same in every picture)
A late-1980s/1990s shopping mall after closing, emptied years ago but with the power still on. One straight
concourse 80 studs wide and 280 long (a player is 5 studs tall, so it is wide and low), a flat suspended ceiling of
square off-white tiles about 3 players high with rows of recessed fluorescent panels: most lit a flat, slightly
green-yellow white, a few dead and one flickering dim. Speckled beige terrazzo floor with a darker border, worn
dull. Walls of yellowed cream plaster with a brown tiled skirting and a faded teal-and-mauve stripe at hand height.
Square columns clad in the same cream. Everything is clean but tired: no rubbish, no graffiti, no cobwebs, no fog,
no puddles, no broken glass. Empty planters with dry soil, a few fixed benches, a dry round fountain in the middle.
Storefronts along both sides, each closed with a grey roll-down shutter stopped half way, dark inside except a faint
glow on the floor just inside. Very few signs, in a plain condensed sans-serif on backlit boxes. The far end is
unlit. The feeling is the liminal one: a familiar ordinary place, completely empty, lit as if someone will come back,
and slightly wrong. Still, silent, a little sad; not gory, not ruined, not "spooky".
