# Codex preliminary visual and layout check

These are proposals from the fresh five Studio MCP Play-camera references, not completed changes or manually walked gameplay verification. Actual Claude independent critique is running separately.

The tunnel is already recognizable. Keep the dominant ochre arch, charcoal road, yellow lane dashes, turquoise curb markers and regularly paired entrances. The largest visual defects are disproportionately bright flat signs, thin disconnected black rib silhouettes, a ceiling wash that turns the concrete mint, and a far furniture wall whose repeated boxes and tipped block props read as procedural noise. The pale far cap is conspicuous behind the pile. More random furniture alone would preserve those defects.

Gate directions can improve while preserving every side entrance. Build a substantial charcoal/yellow jamb-and-lintel facing inward across X, then mount an overhead architectural blade with broad faces toward ±Z. Put its number on a matte cream/yellow face with restrained turquoise trim. The sign blade belongs in the upper sidewalk airspace toward X±26–33; its support must avoid existing benches around X±27.2 and players' access to the portal. Keep the road and curb corridor free. The exact blade dimensions, bottom height and collision need a fresh assembly/Play check before implementation.

The far-stage proposal is geometrically plausible as a schematic. The source inner arch is approximately y=1+sqrt(35²−x²), relative to the builder center floor before shell/service allowances. At x=21 the arch is about29 above that datum; x=22 gives about28.2. Independent floor-mounted towers22–23 high therefore retain approximately5–7 of gross vault clearance at their centers. Check the **outer corners**: a five-stud-wide tower centered at x22 reaches x24.5, where the arch is approximately26 high, leaving only about3 before rib/service allowances. This is not a collision or lighting performance test. A tower mounted on the proposed4–5-high stage would use nearly all of that clearance and should be avoided. Keep the tower bases outside the main lane approach and clear of the Level5/6 row at localZ80. The proposed stage front localZ110 leaves a30-stud center-axis forecourt after that gate row. Hidden service runs and actual stage depth can reduce useful space and must be checked. Existing far pile props around Z125.6 onward overlap the proposed110–128 stage footprint and need deliberate visual redistribution into wings/rear rather than being left beneath the stage.

The DJ subject needs silhouette before buttons: two real-looking deck jog wheels, a central contrasting mixer with sliders, a cable/rack edge, floor subwoofers, and narrow four-chord trusses with actual diagonals. Full fine controls can use one equipment atlas; unbounded individual knobs would add little from spawn. Backroom unease comes from unused hire equipment, warm failing fluorescents, inventory stickers, mismatched black road cases and a few dead display segments. The brief existing Party Mode can animate those lamps without turning the persistent lobby into a rainbow club.

Keep the actual right-wall party-button approach at X32.93/Z108 clear. The ten shop boxes also have functional focus approaches: the current held-focus strip extends roughly X25.58–29.98 along Z−69.45 to−10.55. Gate supports, service boxes and future furniture must stay out of that region. These are source-derived reserved bounds, not successfully exercised interaction tests.

Furniture piles should have designed clusters rather than wall-wide independent random boxes. Heavy file cabinets and desks form stable bases; a copier, one CRT and a grandfather-clock crest make identifiable focal shapes. Wedge chairs deliberately between heavier units, leave shadow gaps between clusters, and hide the solid blocker behind darker cloth/wood/boxes. Keep the resulting blocker sealed. Use restrained wood laminate, dirty cream plastic, olive upholstery, scratched grey steel and paper labels, with wear concentrated at edges and handles. The near pile should be the quieter composition; the far stage can split pile masses into wings and a lower rear storage layer.

Small reusable construction modules can carry most detail: rib foot/joint plates, concrete expansion-seam strips, two inset service panels, one wall ventilation cassette, cable tray, recessed lamp trough and covered electrical box. Tie these to the existing arches and bay intervals. Avoid tiny repeated vents or continuous neon that compete with the gate numerals. No proposed engine limit, FPS result or multiplayer pass is implied.
