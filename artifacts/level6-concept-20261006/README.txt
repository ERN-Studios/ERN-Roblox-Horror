LEVEL 6 - THE ARENA (NEW DRAFT), 2026-10-06

Nothing in the game has changed. These are pictures of a proposal, to say yes or no to.
This replaces the first concept (the hall with zones round the edge).


WHAT IT IS

  One structure, wall to wall. Nothing else.

  Hall            600 x 400 studs  ->  round, 548 studs across
  Roof            46 studs         ->  150 studs
  The frame       10% of the floor ->  95% of the floor
  Floors          3                ->  2 at the court, 12 at the wall
  Other areas     nine             ->  none

  - THE POST stands in a round court in the exact middle, 88 studs across. The Counter stands at it.
  - THE BOWL. The frame goes round the court in six rings. Each ring is two floors taller than the one
    in front of it: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 floors. The top of each ring is a terrace, so the structure
    steps up and away from the post like a stadium, all the way round, and from the front of every
    terrace you look down on the post. Under the terraces the rings are solid frame to hide in.
  - Eight stair aisles go up the terraces like the aisles of a stadium. They are wide enough for the
    Counter, so no floor is out of its reach. Tubes and crawl holes stay too small for it.
  - Tube slides run down the steps, and two net bridges cross the court above the post.
  - YOU ARRIVE in a small tunnel through the wall, right in front of the PLAY ZONE gate. The tunnel is
    8 studs high; the Counter is 8.2 and does not fit. Through the gate one straight lane, 228 studs,
    runs through the structure to the court (14 seconds at a walk). The intro starts when you pass the
    gate, so you walk in with the Counter in view at the far end.
  - Gone: reception, snack shack, party rooms, toddler town, ball ocean, inflatables, staff only,
    arcade and prizes.

FOUR IDEAS FOR THE WAY OUT AFTER THE THIRD TOUCH

  In all four the lights turn red, as they do today, and the way out is the one green light.

  A  THE BIG SLIDE (the one I would build)
     A green light comes on at the very top of the structure, at the mouth of a giant slide that was
     netted off until now. You climb twelve floors with the Counter behind you, dive in, and the slide
     carries you out through the wall. It uses the height, the climb is the chase, and the green light
     can be seen from everywhere. Risk: twelve floors is a long climb; the Counter's speed needs tuning.

  B  BACK THE WAY YOU CAME
     The PLAY ZONE gate opens again and you run for the tunnel, which the Counter cannot enter.
     Cheapest to build and nobody gets lost. Risk: the lane is one long straight line in full view.

  C  UNDER THE POST
     The post sinks into the floor and leaves a chute, one player at a time. Everyone has to come back
     to the middle, the most dangerous spot, and somebody has to draw the Counter away. Needs a delay
     before the hatch opens, or the player who made the third touch is out at once.

  D  A FIRE DOOR
     Four fire doors sit high in the wall at different floors. One unlocks, a different one each time,
     and you have to find the green light from the terraces. Best for replaying; least of a show.

  They can be combined: A as the way out, with B opening as well when the party is down to one.


THE BOWL OR THE WELL (pictures 03, 07 and 16)

  The bowl is the draft. The other way is twelve floors straight up from the edge of the court, like
  the picture of the court you liked but twice as high. It looks taller from the court, but only the
  inner galleries see the post, and it is about 43% more structure.


WHICH PICTURES TO TRUST

  02 and 03 are drawn from the numbers above. Every size in them is exact.
  The rest are generated pictures. They show the scale and the mood. They are not exact: the generator
  tends to draw the rings as one tall wall instead of six steps, floor counts drift, and a few tubes
  change colour along their length. The game will not look as photographic; 11 is the closest to how
  it would look in Roblox.


TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE A BUILD

  1. It is about 26 times as much frame as today (504 cells -> about 13 200). As loose parts that is
     impossible, so it would be built in Blender as one slice of joined meshes repeated eight times
     round the post. I would build one slice first and measure it on a phone before the rest.
  2. Sight. Today the Counter kills what it sees. On open terraces it could see half the level from the
     post, so its sight needs a rule (how far, and whether netting hides you) before the round is fun.
