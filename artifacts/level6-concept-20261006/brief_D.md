These four show four different IDEAS for how the players escape at the end. In the finale every light in the
level turns DEEP RED (the fluorescent strips glow red, the haze is red, the frame is lit red and black) and the
way out is the one thing lit GREEN. Keep that in all four: red everywhere, one green light.

Image `12_escape_A_the_big_slide` (references: refs/liked_from_above.png, refs/liked_court.png)
The way out is at the very TOP. Seen from the court floor looking up the red-lit bowl of terraces: on the
highest terrace, twelve storeys up against the wall, one green lamp glows above the round open mouth of a
gigantic solid-red tube slide. The slide corkscrews down the outside of the top ring for a few turns and then
disappears straight through the hall wall into the dark. Everything else is red and shadow. A stair aisle climbs
all the way up the steps toward the green light. No text.

Image `13_escape_B_back_the_way_you_came` (references: refs/liked_gate.png)
The way out is the way in. Seen from INSIDE the frame at the end of the entrance lane, looking back along it:
the long straight canyon lane between net fences under red light, twelve storeys of frame on both sides, and at
the far end the back of the PLAY ZONE gate standing open, with the small dark hole of the tunnel in the wall
behind it lit green from inside. Sign text, mirrored or from behind is fine; if readable it reads "PLAY ZONE".

Image `14_escape_C_under_the_post` (references: refs/liked_court.png)
The way out is under the post. The court in red light, the terraces rising dark all round. The red padded post

yellow chute, like the mouth of a tube slide, dropping away under the floor, with green light shining up out of
it onto the haze. A few loose balls are rolling toward the hole. No text.

Image `15_escape_D_the_emergency_door` (references: refs/liked_from_above.png, refs/liked_gate.png)
The way out is a fire door high in the wall. Seen from a terrace on the eighth storey under red light: a narrow

open with green-white light in the crack. Below the bridge the frame drops away eight storeys. No other text.
