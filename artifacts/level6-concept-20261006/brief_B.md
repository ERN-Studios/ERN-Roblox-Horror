Image `05_the_spawn_tunnel` (references: refs/liked_gate.png, refs/owner_frame_ingame.png)
Where the players arrive. The camera stands INSIDE the small tunnel through the hall wall: a low, narrow
passage 3 m wide and 2.4 m high, its walls and ceiling lined with worn blue and yellow vinyl crash-padding,
checker foam floor, one weak bulkhead light. Its dark edges frame the picture. A few metres ahead the tunnel
ends at the gateway exactly as in refs/liked_gate.png: padded red-and-yellow posts and the padded blue lintel
reading exactly "PLAY ZONE". Through the gate the lane runs on into the frame, whose netted decks tower twelve
storeys on both sides, far higher than the tunnel mouth can show, and at the far end of the lane, small and
bright, the lit court with the red post. The feeling: you have crawled out of a hole in the wall into something
enormous.

Image `06_the_way_in` (references: refs/liked_gate.png, refs/liked_court.png, refs/doll.png, refs/doll_ingame.png)
Just through the PLAY ZONE gate, eye height, looking straight down the entrance lane: a straight corridor of
checker floor 4 m wide and 65 m long between black net fences. The frame on both sides is twelve storeys high
here and steps DOWN two storeys at a time toward the far end, like the cut edge of a stadium, so the lane is a

the short red post, and beside the post the small distant figure of the Counter standing with its back to us.
Beyond the court the terraces of the far side rise again into the dark. No text.
