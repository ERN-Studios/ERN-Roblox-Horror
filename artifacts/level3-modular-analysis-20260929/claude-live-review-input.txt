# Arkitekturreview: Level 3 → modulært Frutiger Aero-mall

**Status:** Kun lokal, uverificeret kildekode. Ingen live-paritet, visuel vurdering, performance eller tests er påstået. Remote-drift (254f04f) er ikke vurderet.
**[O]** = direkte observeret i koden. **[F]** = forslag.

## 1. Blender-strategi og første kit

**Kontrakt [F]:**
- Roblox genererer shell, collision, navmesh-modifiers, hide-anchors, lyd- og finale-markører og validering, præcis som nu.
- Blender leverer kun visuelle skins, som snappes til navngivne sockets.
- Alle Blender-MeshParts: `CanCollide=false`, `CanQuery=false`, `CanTouch=false`.
- Collision forbliver simple, usynlige Roblox-parts.

**Footprint-katalog [F]:** Erstat de tilfældige integer-mål (W 60–78, D 52–68 [O]) med 3–4 faste footprints på et 4-stud-grid, så de matcher nav-voxlerne. For eksempel:
- 64×56 "butik"
- 72×60 "gade"
- 76×68 "atrium"

Alle skal testes mod `fitRoomTableLayouts`, som asserter ved for lidt plads [O]. GrandBanquet med tre borde er den kritiske sag. Lås rumhøjden til én værdi i stedet for tilfældig 11–13 [O], ellers skal hvert vægpanel findes i tre højder.

**Sockets og pivots [F]:**
- Pivot i gulvcentrum.
- Roblox −Z = frem.
- `Socket_N/E/S/W` i vægcentrum ved gulvniveau.
- Åbningen er præcis `CorridorWidth × CorridorHeight` [O: 14 × 10,5], da rum-åbninger "consume these exact outer dimensions".
- Én fælles eksportskala, verificeret med en testkube via den eksisterende aero-import-pipeline.

**Første kit (ca. 12 assets) [F]:**
1. Vægpanel 8 studs bred, massivt.
2. Vægpanel med portal-insert: afrundet pille-karm i hvid/aqua, uden collision og uden at indsnævre 14×10,5.

## 2. Tilfældige ruter

**Observeret:**
- Hvert distrikt er et 2×4-grid med DFS-træ (7 kanter) plus 2 ekstra af de 3 ubrugte.
- Hver gateway er én enkelt kant, dvs. en bro.
- Alle loops er derfor små og lokale, og distrikter kan ikke omgås.
- Alt trækkes fra én `Random`-strøm: højder, `LocalSeed`, decor-shuffle, links og CD-valg. Ændrer man DecorPool-længden, ændres topologien.

**Forslag:**
- **Tre RNG-strømme:** `topology = Random.new(seed)`, `variant = Random.new(hash(seed,"V"))` og decor pr. rum fra `LocalSeed`. Bump til `GeneratorVersion 3`.
- **Kvalitetsmetrikker i `Validate`,** med afvisning af seeds uden for bånd:
  - korteste sti Arrival→SignalHall i studs,
  - maks. dead-end-dybde,
  - mindst ét loop pr. distrikt, der rører gateway-rummet eller et CD-rum (flugtloop nær mål),
  - CD-spredning langs stien.
- **Læsbarhed:** Ét landmark i hvert gateway-rum og farvekodet skiltning pr. distrikt. De lange N–S-links fungerer som "gallerier" med flush døde butiksfacader. Rækkeafstanden på ±90 [O] giver cirka 112–128 studs mellem rækkerne.

## 3. Hvad bryder AI, hiding og lyd mest

1. **Vertikal clearance.** Korridoren er 10,5 høj og `AgentHeight` er 10 [O]. Enhver nedhængende trim, armatur eller buet soffit bryder Manageren. Lateralt er der kun ca. 2 studs pr. side (14 − 2×5).
2. **Ikke-rektangulære rum.** `Validate` og `corridorBounds` bruger AABB-overlap, og builderen skærer én centreret åbning pr. side [O]. L-rum eller skrå links kræver en ny generator.
3. **LOS-politik.** Hide-occluderen er en usynlig `CanQuery=true`-part [O]. Glossy "glas"-paneler med `CanQuery` vil stille blokere eller slippe sigt. Der skal være en eksplicit regel pr. asset.
4. **Navnebaseret sweep.** `makeRoomProps` stripper parts via `string.find(Name,"Table")` [O]. Blender-navne skal følge en navnekontrakt.
5. **Lyd.** Manifestet kræver præcis 2 scream-markører pr. korridor [O]. Højttalere mixes kun fra de 2 nærmeste, med max-distance 180. Større åbne atrier ændrer dækningen og skal måles.

## 4. Testplan med foreslåede budgetter (ikke garantier)

- **Seed-korpus:** 200 seeds plus fallback-seeds. Mål `Validate`-passrate ≥ 99 %, `Level3_LayoutSeconds` og generation-attempts.
- **Readbacks [O findes]:** `Level3_BuildSeconds` og `Level3_WorldDescendants`. Forslag til mål:
  - build < 6 s,
  - descendants ≤ nuværende baseline −20 %,
  - ≤ 60 unikke MeshIds,
  - ≤ 25 teksturer.
- **MicroProfiler** ved Arrival, største atrium, 560-hallen og under blackout-jagt (PFS-recompute hvert 0,20 s [O]). Server-heartbeat skal være stabil med 6 spillere.
- **Low-end mobil:** klient-frametid og hukommelse (GraphicsMeshParts/Texture). Mål netværksspidsen ved én-gangs-publiceringen, da verden bygges i ServerStorage og parentes samlet [O].
- **Automatiseret clearance-test:** Sweep en kapsel med radius 5,25 og højde 10 gennem alle korridorer og rumperimetre pr. seed.

## 5. Fase 1

En femrums-slice er umulig i den nuværende kode, fordi `Validate` hardkræver 26 rum og 31 links, og modulet asserter grid-størrelsen ved load [O].

**Anbefaling [F]:** Behold den fulde generator og skin kun District 1 plus Arrival med kittet bag et flag. Så testes CD #1, hiding, blackout og gateway-overgangen mod ægte topologi. Et komplet redesign bør først starte, når clearance-testen og budgetterne holder.

## 6. Misvisende antagelser i den foreløbige review

- **"6 cycles":** Aritmetisk korrekt (31−26+1), men alle er intra-distrikt, og alle gateways er broer. Der er ingen distriktsomgående loops.
- **"By defaults":** Generatorens `DEFAULTS` afviger fra `Configuration.Layout`, f.eks. RowHalfSpacing 62 mod 90 og depth 48–62 mod 52–68. `Master.Overlay("L3Layout")` kan ændre værdierne ved hver `Generate`. Live-værdier er ukendte.
- **"14-stud corridor":** Højden på 10,5 er den bindende grænse, ikke bredden. Korridorlængderne er stærkt anisotrope.
- **"Current AI assumes axis-aligned":** AI-koden er ikke vist. Observeret er kun, at generator og builder antager det.
- **"Blender only visuals":** Visuals påvirker stadig navmesh, LOS, navne-sweeps og lys. Det er ikke passivt.
- **"One-way/locked doors":** Den nuværende revision har ingen døre ud over HiddenExit [O], og BFS er urettet. Det er nyt scope, ikke noget der skal bevares.
- **Manager-spawn:** SignalHall er kun fallback. Den reelle spawn vælges nær den tætteste spillergruppe [O, kommentar].

## Centrale ingeniørbeslutninger

1. **Socket-kontrakt:** Blender = ikke-kolliderende, ikke-querybare skins. Roblox = topologi, collision, nav, LOS og markører. Håndhæves af navnekonvention og en automatiseret clearance-sweep.
2. **Behold 2×4-grafen i v3,** men med faste 4-stud-footprints, separate RNG-strømme og graf-kvalitetsmetrikker i `Validate`, før der modelleres geometri.
3. **Fastlås korridor- og rumhøjde først.** Hæv korridoren til ≥ 12, eller forbyd al trim under 10,5 + margin. Hele kittets mål afhænger af den beslutning.

209: local function cardinalSide(a, b)
210: 	local dx, dz = b.X - a.X, b.Z - a.Z
211: 	if math.abs(dz) < 0.001 and math.abs(dx) >= 0.001 then
212: 		return if dx > 0 then "East" else "West"
213: 	end
214: 	if math.abs(dx) < 0.001 and math.abs(dz) >= 0.001 then
215: 		return if dz > 0 then "South" else "North"
216: 	end
217: 	return nil
218: end

227: local function planarDistance(a, b)
228: 	local dx, dz = a.X - b.X, a.Z - b.Z
229: 	return math.sqrt(dx * dx + dz * dz)

232: local function measureBounds(rooms, excludeExit)
233: 	local minX, maxX, minZ, maxZ = math.huge, -math.huge, math.huge, -math.huge
234: 	for _, room in ipairs(rooms) do
235: 		if not excludeExit or room.Id ~= "Exit" then
236: 			minX = math.min(minX, room.X - room.W * .5)
237: 			maxX = math.max(maxX, room.X + room.W * .5)
238: 			minZ = math.min(minZ, room.Z - room.D * .5)
239: 			maxZ = math.max(maxZ, room.Z + room.D * .5)
240: 		end
241: 	end
242: 	local width, depth = maxX - minX, maxZ - minZ
243: 	return {MinX=minX, MaxX=maxX, MinZ=minZ, MaxZ=maxZ, Width=width, Depth=depth,
244: 		Aspect=math.max(width, depth) / math.min(width, depth)}
245: end

247: local function boundsMatch(actual, expected)
248: 	if type(actual) ~= "table" then return false end
249: 	for key, value in pairs(expected) do
250: 		if type(actual[key]) ~= "number" or actual[key] ~= actual[key]
251: 			or math.abs(actual[key]) == math.huge or math.abs(actual[key] - value) > .001 then return false end
252: 	end
253: 	return true
254: end

256: local function rectanglesOverlap(a, b)
257: 	return math.min(a.MaxX, b.MaxX) - math.max(a.MinX, b.MinX) > .001
258: 		and math.min(a.MaxZ, b.MaxZ) - math.max(a.MinZ, b.MinZ) > .001

261: -- Conservative footprint of the actual corridor kit: the 14-stud clear lane
262: -- plus both side walls, and the .04 floor seal. Endpoint rooms own the joins.
263: local function corridorBounds(a, b)
264: 	local halfWidth = Configuration.CorridorWidth * .5 + Configuration.WallThickness
265: 	if math.abs(a.Z - b.Z) < .001 then
266: 		local left, right = if a.X < b.X then a else b, if a.X < b.X then b else a
267: 		return {MinX=left.X + left.W * .5 - .02, MaxX=right.X - right.W * .5 + .02,
268: 			MinZ=a.Z - halfWidth, MaxZ=a.Z + halfWidth}
269: 	end
270: 	local north, south = if a.Z < b.Z then a else b, if a.Z < b.Z then b else a
271: 	return {MinX=a.X - halfWidth, MaxX=a.X + halfWidth,
272: 		MinZ=north.Z + north.D * .5 - .02, MaxZ=south.Z - south.D * .5 + .02}
273: end

275: -- Room and tunnel openings are centred on each linked side. This is the
276: -- longest uninterrupted structural view through those openings, excluding
277: -- the deliberately separate Signal Hall -> Exit finale.
278: local function straightRuns(layout)
279: 	local ports = {}
280: 	for _, room in ipairs(layout.Rooms) do ports[room.Id] = {} end
281: 	for _, link in ipairs(layout.Links) do
282: 		if link.Door ~= "HiddenExit" then
283: 			local a, b = layout.RoomById[link.A], layout.RoomById[link.B]
284: 			local side = cardinalSide(a, b)
285: 			if side then
286: 				ports[a.Id][side] = b.Id
287: 				ports[b.Id][OPPOSITE_SIDE[side]] = a.Id
288: 			end
289: 		end
290: 	end
291: 	local longest, vertical = 0, 0
292: 	for roomId, sides in pairs(ports) do
293: 		for _, side in ipairs({"East", "South"}) do
294: 			if sides[side] and not sides[OPPOSITE_SIDE[side]] then
295: 				local count, current = 0, roomId
296: 				while ports[current][side] do
297: 					count += 1
298: 					current = ports[current][side]
299: 				end
300: 				longest = math.max(longest, count)
301: 				if side == "South" then vertical = math.max(vertical, count) end
302: 			end
303: 		end
304: 	end
305: 	return longest, vertical
306: end

308: local function makeHash(layout)
309: 	local pieces = {
310: 		tostring(VERSION),
311: 		tostring(layout.Seed),
312: 		tostring(layout.Attempt),
313: 	}
314: 	for _, room in ipairs(layout.Rooms) do
315: 		table.insert(pieces, string.format(
316: 			"R:%s:%.3f:%.3f:%d:%d:%d:%s:%s:%s:%d:%d:%d",
317: 			room.Id, room.X, room.Z, room.W, room.D, room.H,
318: 			room.Kind, room.Decor, room.ThemeId, room.SectionIndex,
319: 			if room.Module then 1 else 0, room.LocalSeed
320: 		))
321: 	end
322: 	for _, link in ipairs(layout.Links) do
323: 		table.insert(pieces, string.format(
324: 			"L:%s:%s:%s:%s:%s:%d:%d",
325: 			link.Id, link.A, link.B, link.Door, link.ThemeId,
326: 			link.SectionIndex, if link.Gateway then 1 else 0
327: 		))
328: 	end
329: 	local text = table.concat(pieces, ";")
330: 	local hash = 5381
331: 	for index = 1, #text do
332: 		hash = (hash * 33 + string.byte(text, index)) % 4294967296
333: 	end
334: 	return string.format("L3-%d-%08x", VERSION, hash)
335: end

569: 	local function neighbourSlots(sectionIndex, row, column)
570: 		local result = {}
571: 		local current = slots[sectionIndex][row][column]
572: 		local function allow(other)
573: 			if pairKey(current.Id, other.Id) ~= omitted[sectionIndex] then
574: 				table.insert(result, other)
575: 			end
576: 		end
577: 		if row > 1 then allow(slots[sectionIndex][row - 1][column]) end
578: 		if row < GRID_ROWS then allow(slots[sectionIndex][row + 1][column]) end
579: 		if column > 1 then allow(slots[sectionIndex][row][column - 1]) end
580: 		if column < GRID_COLUMNS then allow(slots[sectionIndex][row][column + 1]) end
581: 		return result

826: function LayoutGenerator.Validate(layout)
827: 	if type(layout) ~= "table" then return fail("layout is not a table") end
828: 	if layout.Version ~= VERSION then return fail("layout version is stale") end
829: 	if type(layout.Rooms) ~= "table" or #layout.Rooms ~= DISTRICT_COUNT * ROOMS_PER_DISTRICT + 2 then
830: 		return fail("layout must contain exactly 26 rooms")
831: 	end
832: 	local expectedLinks = DISTRICT_COUNT
833: 		* ((ROOMS_PER_DISTRICT - 1) + Tuning.ExtraLinksPerDistrict)
834: 		+ ENTRY_AND_GATEWAY_LINKS
835: 	if type(layout.Links) ~= "table" or #layout.Links ~= expectedLinks then
836: 		return fail(string.format("layout must contain exactly %d links", expectedLinks))
837: 	end
838: 	if type(layout.Districts) ~= "table" or #layout.Districts ~= DISTRICT_COUNT then
839: 		return fail("layout must contain exactly three districts")
840: 	end
841: 	if type(layout.RoomById) ~= "table" or type(layout.Adjacency) ~= "table"
842: 		or type(layout.GatewayLinks) ~= "table" then
843: 		return fail("layout lookup tables are missing")
844: 	end

846: 	local seenRooms = {}
847: 	local sectionCounts = {0, 0, 0}
848: 	local moduleCounts = {0, 0, 0}
849: 	local moduleTotal = 0
850: 	local hideSpotTotal = 0
851: 	for _, room in ipairs(layout.Rooms) do
852: 		if type(room.Id) ~= "string" or room.Id == "" or seenRooms[room.Id] then
853: 			return fail("room ids must be non-empty and unique")
854: 		end
855: 		seenRooms[room.Id] = true
856: 		if layout.RoomById[room.Id] ~= room then return fail("RoomById is inconsistent for " .. room.Id) end
857: 		if type(room.X) ~= "number" or type(room.Z) ~= "number"
858: 			or type(room.W) ~= "number" or type(room.D) ~= "number" or type(room.H) ~= "number"
859: 			or room.W <= 0 or room.D <= 0 or room.H <= 0 then
860: 			return fail("room dimensions are invalid for " .. room.Id)
861: 		end
862: 		if room.Id == "Arrival" or room.Id == "Exit" then
863: 			if room.HideSpotCount ~= 0 or room.Module == true then
864: 				return fail(room.Id .. " must not have hide spots or a module")
865: 			end
866: 		else
867: 			if room.SectionIndex < 1 or room.SectionIndex > DISTRICT_COUNT then
868: 				return fail("district room has invalid section: " .. room.Id)
869: 			end
870: 			if room.HideSpotCount ~= 1 then
871: 				return fail("each district room must request exactly one hide spot")
872: 			end
873: 			hideSpotTotal += room.HideSpotCount
874: 			sectionCounts[room.SectionIndex] += 1
875: 			local definition = DISTRICT_DEFINITIONS[room.SectionIndex]
876: 			if room.ThemeId ~= definition.ThemeId or room.Kind ~= definition.Kind
877: 				or room.DistrictId ~= definition.Id then
878: 				return fail("district theme metadata is inconsistent for " .. room.Id)
879: 			end
880: 			if room.Module == true then
881: 				moduleTotal += 1
882: 				moduleCounts[room.SectionIndex] += 1
883: 			end
884: 		end
885: 	end
886: 	if not seenRooms.Arrival or not seenRooms.Exit or not seenRooms.SignalHall then
887: 		return fail("Arrival, SignalHall, and Exit are required")
888: 	end
889: 	for sectionIndex = 1, DISTRICT_COUNT do
890: 		if sectionCounts[sectionIndex] ~= ROOMS_PER_DISTRICT then
891: 			return fail("every district must contain exactly eight rooms")
892: 		end
893: 		if moduleCounts[sectionIndex] < 1 then
894: 			return fail("every district must contain at least one module")
895: 		end
896: 	end
897: 	if moduleTotal ~= MODULE_GOAL or type(layout.ModuleRooms) ~= "table"
898: 		or #layout.ModuleRooms ~= MODULE_GOAL then
899: 		return fail("layout must contain exactly five module rooms")
900: 	end
901: 	if hideSpotTotal ~= DISTRICT_COUNT * ROOMS_PER_DISTRICT then
902: 		return fail("layout must request exactly 24 evenly distributed hide spots")
903: 	end
904: 
905: 	for index = 1, #layout.Rooms - 1 do
906: 		local a = layout.Rooms[index]
907: 		for otherIndex = index + 1, #layout.Rooms do
908: 			local b = layout.Rooms[otherIndex]
909: 			local overlapX = (a.W + b.W) * 0.5 - math.abs(a.X - b.X)
910: 			local overlapZ = (a.D + b.D) * 0.5 - math.abs(a.Z - b.Z)
911: 			if overlapX > 0.001 and overlapZ > 0.001 then
912: 				return fail("rooms overlap: " .. a.Id .. " and " .. b.Id)
913: 			end
914: 		end
915: 	end

917: 	local ports = {}
918: 	local seenPairs = {}
919: 	local hiddenCount = 0
920: 	local gatewayCount = 0
921: 	local corridorFootprints = {}
922: 	local rebuiltAdjacency = {}
923: 	for roomId in pairs(seenRooms) do
924: 		ports[roomId] = {}
925: 		rebuiltAdjacency[roomId] = {}
926: 	end
927: 	for _, link in ipairs(layout.Links) do
928: 		local a, b = layout.RoomById[link.A], layout.RoomById[link.B]
929: 		if not a or not b or a == b then return fail("link endpoint is invalid") end
930: 		local key = pairKey(a.Id, b.Id)
931: 		if seenPairs[key] then return fail("duplicate room pair " .. key) end
932: 		seenPairs[key] = true
933: 		local sideA = cardinalSide(a, b)
934: 		if not sideA then return fail("link is not straight and axis-aligned: " .. key) end
935: 		local sideB = OPPOSITE_SIDE[sideA]
936: 		if ports[a.Id][sideA] or ports[b.Id][sideB] then
937: 			return fail("more than one connection uses the same room side: " .. key)
938: 		end
939: 		ports[a.Id][sideA] = link.Id
940: 		ports[b.Id][sideB] = link.Id
941: 		local gap
942: 		if sideA == "East" or sideA == "West" then
943: 			gap = math.abs(a.X - b.X) - (a.W + b.W) * 0.5
944: 		else
945: 			gap = math.abs(a.Z - b.Z) - (a.D + b.D) * 0.5
946: 		end
947: 		if gap < Tuning.MinimumCorridorLength - 0.001 then
948: 			return fail("corridor is shorter than the configured minimum: " .. key)
949: 		end
950: 		table.insert(rebuiltAdjacency[a.Id], b.Id)
951: 		table.insert(rebuiltAdjacency[b.Id], a.Id)
952: 		if link.Door == "HiddenExit" then
953: 			hiddenCount += 1
954: 			if not (link.A == "SignalHall" and link.B == "Exit")
955: 				or math.abs(gap - Tuning.ExitCorridorLength) > .001 then
956: 				return fail("the only hidden link must be SignalHall to Exit")
957: 			end
958: 		end
959: 		if link.Gateway == true then
960: 			gatewayCount += 1
961: 			local kind = if a.SectionIndex == 0 then "Entry"
962: 				elseif b.Id == "Exit" then "Exit" else "District"
963: 			if link.FromSection ~= a.SectionIndex or link.ToSection ~= b.SectionIndex
964: 				or b.SectionIndex ~= a.SectionIndex + 1
965: 				or link.FromThemeId ~= a.ThemeId or link.ToThemeId ~= b.ThemeId
966: 				or link.GatewayKind ~= kind or layout.GatewayLinks[gatewayCount] ~= link then
967: 				return fail("gateway metadata is inconsistent: " .. key)
968: 			end
969: 			if kind == "District" then
970: 				if sideA ~= "South" or a.GridRow ~= GRID_ROWS or b.GridRow ~= 1
971: 					or a.GridColumn ~= b.GridColumn or type(layout.GatewayColumns) ~= "table"
972: 					or layout.GatewayColumns[a.SectionIndex] ~= a.GridColumn then
973: 					return fail("district gateway does not join the shared column: " .. key)
974: 				end
975: 			elseif sideA ~= "East" then
976: 				return fail("arrival and final exit must face east")
977: 			end
978: 		end
979: 		if a.SectionIndex == b.SectionIndex and a.SectionIndex >= 1
980: 			and a.SectionIndex <= DISTRICT_COUNT then
981: 			local definition = DISTRICT_DEFINITIONS[a.SectionIndex]
982: 			if link.ThemeId ~= definition.ThemeId or link.SectionIndex ~= a.SectionIndex then
983: 				return fail("internal link theme does not match its district")
984: 			end
985: 		end
986: 		table.insert(corridorFootprints, {Link=link, Bounds=corridorBounds(a, b)})
987: 	end
988: 	if hiddenCount ~= 1 then return fail("layout must contain one hidden exit link") end
989: 	if gatewayCount ~= ENTRY_AND_GATEWAY_LINKS
990: 		or type(layout.GatewayLinks) ~= "table"
991: 		or #layout.GatewayLinks ~= ENTRY_AND_GATEWAY_LINKS then
992: 		return fail("layout must contain four explicit gateway links")
993: 	end
994: 	if type(layout.GatewayColumns) ~= "table" or #layout.GatewayColumns ~= 2 then
995: 		return fail("layout must identify two gateway columns")
996: 	end
997: 	for index, corridor in ipairs(corridorFootprints) do
998: 		for _, room in ipairs(layout.Rooms) do
999: 			if room.Id ~= corridor.Link.A and room.Id ~= corridor.Link.B then
1000: 				local halfWall = Configuration.WallThickness * .5
1001: 				local roomShell = {MinX=room.X-room.W*.5-halfWall, MaxX=room.X+room.W*.5+halfWall,
1002: 					MinZ=room.Z-room.D*.5-halfWall, MaxZ=room.Z+room.D*.5+halfWall}
1003: 				if rectanglesOverlap(corridor.Bounds, roomShell) then
1004: 					return fail("corridor crosses unrelated room: " .. corridor.Link.Id .. " and " .. room.Id)
1005: 				end
1006: 			end
1007: 		end

1009: 			local other = corridorFootprints[otherIndex]
1010: 			if rectanglesOverlap(corridor.Bounds, other.Bounds) then
1011: 				return fail("corridor shells overlap: " .. corridor.Link.Id .. " and " .. other.Link.Id)
1012: 			end
1013: 		end
1014: 	end
1015: 	if not boundsMatch(layout.Bounds, measureBounds(layout.Rooms))
1016: 		or not boundsMatch(layout.CoreBounds, measureBounds(layout.Rooms, true)) then
1017: 		return fail("layout bounds are stale")
1018: 	end

1020: 	for roomId, expected in pairs(rebuiltAdjacency) do
1021: 		table.sort(expected)
1022: 		local actual = layout.Adjacency[roomId]
1023: 		if type(actual) ~= "table" or #actual ~= #expected then
1024: 			return fail("Adjacency is inconsistent for " .. roomId)
1025: 		end
1026: 		for index, otherId in ipairs(expected) do

1038: 			if not reached[otherId] then

1040: 				table.insert(queue, otherId)
1041: 			end
1042: 		end
1043: 	end

1046: 	end

1048: 	if layout.MaximumStraightRun ~= straight or layout.MaximumVerticalRun ~= vertical then
1049: 		return fail("straight-run diagnostics are stale")

1052: 		if straight > Tuning.MaximumStraightRunLinks
1053: 			or vertical > Tuning.MaximumVerticalRunLinks then
1054: 			return fail("core has an excessively long straight view")
1055: 		end
1056: 		for _, otherId in ipairs(layout.Adjacency.SignalHall) do
1057: 			local other = layout.RoomById[otherId]

1059: 				return fail("Signal Hall opens onto the finale axis")
1060: 			end

1064: 	for sectionIndex = 1, DISTRICT_COUNT do
1065: 		local district = layout.Districts[sectionIndex]
1066: 		if district.Index ~= sectionIndex or district.Id ~= DISTRICT_DEFINITIONS[sectionIndex].Id
