The original GameManager owns a private `lobbyStations` registry, `runStation` worker, ConfigureQueue admission, capacity collisions, countdown, cancellation and launch. There is no RoundService registration API. Cosmetic preview prompts cannot call private original callbacks, and sending ConfigureQueue from an unregistered pad fails its host/position checks. The simplest complete integration is a guarded registration hook inside this same engine plus the isolated QueueBridge mapping module. It adds twelve real stations101–112 for levels1–3; the original IDs/rows/jobs/spawn remain intact. RoundUI accepts arbitrary numeric station IDs unchanged. No client queue UI change is required.

The new module lives at tools/lobby_reimagined/queue_bridge.ModuleScript.luau, installed by the owner under ServerScriptService.LobbyReimaginedPreview.QueueBridge. It validates an explicitly owned ready revision3 model, all24 uniquely numbered zone descriptors, radius/nonphysical flags, direct bay parent/floor/diameter and each separate render owner/title/subtitle. Levels4–6 descriptors are previewOnly. The companion GameManager candidate adds the lifecycle hook and exact cleanup, including a retirement flag checked during countdown and before launch. Existing launch/teleport logic is reused unchanged. This candidate is a local proposal pending fresh root CAS, not proof of Studio installation.

Zones must be direct children of their bay Model with ChamberFloor at the bay center and CircularBayDiameter. Each zone has R3QueueId, LevelNumber, QueueDisplayIndex, QueueDetectorShape=Circle and finite positive QueueRadius bounded by half its Size.X/Z. ObjectValue QueueRenderOwner references the pad's own hologram/status Model; QueueTitle and QueueSubtitle are descendant TextLabels. Publishing QueueActive on the shared bay would light all four holograms together; instead the station renderOwner reflects true host/reset/loading state. The existing physical full-party barrier/collision authority remains separate.

ConfigureQueue's phone cancellation moves to zone-local−Z by half-depth+5. Four pads around a bay therefore need zone LookVector toward bay center. A common yaw causes the rear-row host to be cancelled directly into a neighboring front-row queue. An outward yaw approaches the room wall too closely. The geometry validator must measure every actual cancel point against all pad radii and solid walls. Visual HologramBase can retain its own orientation independently.
