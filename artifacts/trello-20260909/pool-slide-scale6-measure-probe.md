## What the numbers mean

`GetBoundingBox` covers BasePart boxes, so it is reported only as `staticBox`. Animated `Bone.TransformedWorldCFrame` supplies real poses. For visible MeshParts, the probe attempts read-only access to their existing skin vertices, bind frames and weights. It matches unique bone names, derives uniform scale from bind distances, requires common rest transforms within 0.02 studs, normalized weights, complete vertices, and rest vertices inside the MeshPart box within 0.05 studs. Unsupported mappings fail that mesh; no guessed import units or mesh origin are used. It never creates or publishes a mesh asset.

With `vertexCoverage=true`, `sampledSkin` is a **reconstructed linear-blend skin envelope at sampled engine poses**, not a renderer readback or a continuous-motion proof. Validate the reconstruction visually at rest and extreme poses. With any mesh failure, only partial skin and `boneProxy` remain; neither proves the whole entity fits. Record the specific read/mapping error. Account/asset permissions must not be changed by this probe.

Radius is the maximum distance from the model pivot in XZ, so that scalar already covers every yaw orientation for each sampled pose. `minY/maxY` retain pivot offset information. Suggested minimum agent dimensions include the current controller's 0.1 horizontal and 0.3 vertical margins, and height above the static sole plane as well as total span. These are lower bounds from samples, not values to copy blindly. Repeat denser sampling near extrema and inspect transitions/turns before setting envelope attributes.

Foot groups use vertices with at least 0.75 influence from a selected foot bone; otherwise the output explicitly says `bone-proxy`. `minimumPlaneGap` includes the navigator's 0.08 floor clearance and GroundOffset derived from the cloned static box. Low-phase-height intervals within 0.15 studs suggest stance. Their positive local-Z velocity predicts the forward -Z speed that would plant the foot. `candidateReferenceSpeed` is a median of those intervals; inspect the raw samples because an authored foot, toe roll or attack can invalidate that heuristic. Idle/Attack velocities are diagnostic only. Current-config predicted slip and candidate recalibrated rates are given for Walk10/Run20/Run32. This static-stage calculation does not prove a moving NPC's soles are planted.

Current scale 4 references are 8.0622053/17.3676065. Pure 1.5× scaling predicts about 12.093308/26.051410 at scale 6; corresponding rates at 10/20/32 are 0.8269/0.7677/1.2283. These predictions are comparison values, not new measurements or runtime changes.
