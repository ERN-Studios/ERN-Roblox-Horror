@everyone We've shipped changes to **BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET**!

- Fixed several lobby and level-entry issues, including party spawn spacing and the Level 3 slide entrance. The Level 3 exit now works by walking into it. Lengthened the Level 3 slide and staggered successive spawns higher up.
- Full lobby circles now move extra players safely outside while keeping the existing party in place.
- Carried fuses and CDs now drop where you die so other players can pick them up.
- Restored the 15-second auto-continue between levels, with each player's choice visible on the results screen. You can now switch between Continue and Return to Lobby until the transition starts.
- Added **Entity Shield** under Upgrades: spend **5 Research Tokens** for a stored charge, then activate it when you need **5 seconds of protection**.
- Completing a level now awards **2 Research Tokens**.
- Refreshed the Shop, Upgrades buttons, and product/pass artwork. The Level 1 exit arrow now has a compass display, with clearer placement on touch screens.
- Removed the elevator's forced camera movement and improved the Level 1 chase audio.
- Fixed Pool Slide attacks near walls and corners, and added a lobby music toggle.

Thanks for the bug reports and feedback. Keep posting anything you find in the bug and feedback channels!
