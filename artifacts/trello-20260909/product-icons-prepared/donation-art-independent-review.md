# Six donation masters — independent visual review

Reviewer: `/root/spawn_diagnosis`. **9/10 for the proposed master artwork family.** All six proposals meet the requested minimum of 8; no regeneration is required by this review.

I opened each actual 1254px PNG with `view_image`, read the six prompts, final manifest and artwork README, and checked the current donation config/receipt scope. The donation products record voluntary support and do not grant the depicted objects, tokens, re-entry credits, character gear or a role. The current Donate UI is text-only; this artwork review does not approve adding image cards or changing grants.

The six images fit the existing graphite, teal and brass family and differ clearly from the Shop's token packs, equipment and re-entry door. Their decorative heart emblems work in the explicit donation context; they contain no medical cross, extra-life count, upgrade arrow or numeric health promise. Do not reuse them as gameplay-health or granted-badge illustrations.

The full-master silhouettes appear safe within the full inscribed circular crop. Signal, Supply, Research and Command use much of the available diameter; their actual borders visibly retain space from the square edge, but they do not all satisfy the prompts' stricter central 70–80% framing target. This is not an observed clipped master. The actual 512px export and 76px/64px circular rendering still need inspection; I did not render a crop, open the HTML/browser or claim native small-size acceptance. Keep the heart and complete outer frame when exporting.

All six master SHA256 values match `art-manifest.json`.
