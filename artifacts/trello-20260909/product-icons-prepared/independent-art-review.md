Reviewer: `/root/spawn_diagnosis`. **9/10 for the six proposed master artworks**, before export, upload and native Shop acceptance. No required regeneration was identified in this bounded review.

I opened all six actual 1254px masters through `view_image`, read `art-prompts.json` and `art-manifest.json`, and compared the motifs with the current `ZyntraConfig` grants/descriptions and the existing Shop image consumer. I did not open a browser, operate Studio, alter images or upload anything. The checked files are the six `*-v2.png` entries in the manifest; their SHA256 values match the files.

The family is coherent: broad beveled industrial forms, graphite, teal and restrained warm metal, with color used for category. The meaningful silhouettes visibly have space around them within the square and appear compatible with the full inscribed circular crop. Some forms occupy more than the prompt's stricter central 80%-diameter target, particularly the wider token cache; this is not an observed cut-off in the master. A native circle at the actual 76px Shop size and the intended 64px platform preview remains necessary after export. I did **not** render or certify those small-size circular previews during this read-only review.

Use the existing labels/descriptions for precise quantities and entitlements. Export the approved masters to 512px, preserve the original sources, and verify real circular cropping/contrast at final size before publishing assignments.
