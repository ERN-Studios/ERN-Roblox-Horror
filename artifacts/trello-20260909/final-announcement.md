@everyone We've shipped changes to **BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET**!

- Fixed lobby and level-entry issues, including party spawn spacing and the Level 3 slide entrance. The Level 3 exit works by walking into it. The slide is longer, with successive spawns placed higher up.
- Full lobby circles move extra players safely outside while keeping the existing party in place.
- Carried fuses and CDs now drop on death for other players to recover.
- The results screen keeps its 15-second auto-continue and shows each player's choice. You can switch between Continue and Return to Lobby until the transition starts.
- **Entity Shield** is under Upgrades: spend **5 Research Tokens** for a stored charge, then activate **5 seconds of protection** when needed.
- Completing a level now awards **2 Research Tokens**.
- Refreshed the section and product/pass artwork. The Level 1 exit pointer has a compass display, and the Stamina/Battery descriptions are shorter.
- Removed forced elevator camera movement, improved Level 1 chase audio, fixed Pool Slide attacks near walls and corners, and added a lobby music toggle.
- Five ambient sweep patterns now move across the lobby ceiling lights.
- Shop and Upgrades now use square buttons with clear two-color icons.

Thanks for the bug reports and feedback. Keep posting anything you find in the bug and feedback channels!
