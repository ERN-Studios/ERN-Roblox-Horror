## Material limits

The native tests run real server/client scheduling and gameplay against an isolated memory profile backend. Live DataStore service behaviour and a real paid purchase were not tested, and no such claim is needed here. The actual-source persistence tests cover conflict, retry, lost response, lease/rejoin and refund paths. The acknowledged crash boundary remains: an effect delivered before its durable completion can result in a single refund on a later owner recovery.

L2/L3 target, observer, impact and mixed-player behaviour has substantial actual-source coverage, including teammate/world-noise preservation and stale identity fences, but no physical multiplayer/native L3 acceptance is claimed. Physical phone/gamepad use and every AI combination were not exhaustively exercised. These are remaining QA risks, not newly demonstrated blockers requiring a general test framework before this release.
