BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET [CO-OP HORROR]
THE SIX LEVELS AS THEY ARE ON 2026-10-07: what each looks like, what hunts you, how you clear it

Written by Claude after going into every level in Studio the same evening (one player, full graphics quality).
The 42 pictures in "In-game references" were taken on that run; every promo picture was made from them.
Each level says what was done on this run and what is known from earlier verified runs or from the game's code.


THE PLAYER (the same in every level)
A chunky little figure about three heads tall: mustard-yellow hooded hazmat suit of crumpled fabric, a black
gas mask with two round lenses and one round filter, a black tactical backpack with two small olive tanks,
black belt with pouches, black mitten gloves, chunky black boots. A hand torch, a sprint that makes noise,
a crouch. Everyone in a party looks the same.
NOTE FOR THE OLD ARTWORK: the covers of 2026-10-04 drew "blocky avatars with dark visors". The real suit has a
twin-lens gas mask and a backpack. This batch uses the real one.


LEVEL 1, THE OFFICE  (a round; entity: the Entity)
Look     The yellow backrooms: mustard damask wallpaper, beige-brown carpet, low white tile ceiling with
         fluorescent panels, square pillars, a 40 x 40 maze that is different every round, black marker
         scribbles on the walls ("THE CARPET MOVES"), a red line on the carpet from the elevator, office
         chairs and boxes. You arrive in a brushed-steel elevator with a floor panel 1 to 8.
Entity   Tall, hunched and pitch black, in a rough hooded knit garment; heavy shoulders, very long arms, big
         long-fingered hands, chunky feet. Two small round eyes: amber when calm, RED when it is hunting. It
         hunts by sight and by sound (sprinting is loud, sneaking is silent, a torch lets it see you further).
         It pounces, and a kill is five seconds on the floor seen through your own eyes.
To clear 1. Pull fuses out of the wall cabinets marked ZYNTRA RELAY (pulling one makes noise).
         2. Carry each to a fuse box. Every fuse raises the danger; all boxes full = a red ALERT.
         3. Pull every lever within 10 seconds of each other.
         4. The power goes down in a wave, the exit gate opens, and the Entity guards it. Get out.
         Each player in the party adds one fuse box, one lever and one coloured cable to follow.
This run A real round: elevator ride, into the maze, the Entity walked 400 studs across the maze to within
         110 studs of me and was photographed from 15 and 20 studs. Relays, fuse box, lever and exit were
         located in the round. The fuse run itself was not played tonight; it is described from the game's
         README and from earlier rounds.


LEVEL 2, THE POOLROOMS  (BRAND NEW MAP; a walk; NO ENTITY)
Look     Nothing like the old poolrooms. A silent, sunlit, clean bath-house of impossible size, six areas:
         1  a flooded hall of tiled columns and low vaults over turquoise water, a dark machine at its end
         3  a nave of colossal fluted concrete columns on tiled plinths, a sunken pool between them
         4  an immense stepwell: zig-zag stairs with glass rails and arched doors, tier under tier, down to a
            green pool, one slender pillar up the middle, a stair tower beside it
         2  a domed rotunda in deep blue and gold mosaic, a round pool, a golden gong on an island, a deck chair
         5  a lap-pool hall under a truss roof with ribbon windows onto empty desert
         6  a ten-storey atrium of identical yellow doors on galleries over a drained pool, a rusted spiral
            stair up to the last gallery, where the way out is
         (The areas are numbered as the map names them; the route visits them 1, 3, 4, 2, 5, 6.)
Entity   None. No creature, no foam, nothing. The horror is the size and the silence.
To clear Walk the route from the start stairs through the six areas to the EXIT at the top of area 6.
         It is a developer preview today: entered from a pedestal in the lobby's Level 2 bay, with RETURN TO
         LOBBY at the start and at the EXIT. No round, no objective, no timer yet.
This run Stood in all six areas and at the exit (moved from marker to marker, not walked end to end).
         9 pictures. The old Level 2 pictures (tube slides, the Pool Foam figures) are out of date and were
         not used anywhere in this batch.


LEVEL 3, THE PARTY ROOMS  (a round; entity: the Mall Manager)
Look     The children's party rooms of a dead mall: road-map play carpet in some rooms, dark confetti carpet
         in others, cream walls with toy stickers or orange walls with a black lower band, a black tile
         ceiling with a few light panels, folding tables with paper cups and plates, plastic kids' chairs,
         bunches of balloons, an arcade corner with a prize claw.
Entity   Very tall and thin. Its head is a glossy RED BALLOON with no face. Short-sleeved shirt in wide orange
         and cream stripes with a white name tag, black trousers, white belt buckle, black shoes, long thin
         pale arms ending in red gloves. It is NOT in the level at the start: it comes with the blackout.
         It goes for the nearest living player even when they are hidden, walks to their table and checks it.
To clear 1. Find the five PARTY MIX CDs (jewel cases on folding tables). You can hide UNDER any folding table,
            two players to a table.
         2. Carry them to the BIRTHDAY CD PLAYER in the Signal Hall and put them in.
         3. The music plays, then the lights go: the blackout. The Manager hunts. Hide, and when it checks
            your table you have about two seconds to get out the other side.
         4. The exit opens: a 560-stud hall. Once every survivor is past half way the Manager appears at the
            hall's entrance and comes at 28 against your walking 16. Keep moving. Stopping is death.
This run A real round was started: the start room, a CD on its table, the 30 tables, the CD player and the
         arcade were located. The Manager was photographed from the game's own model, stood in a party room,
         because it does not exist until the blackout. The whole clear was played on 2026-10-05.


LEVEL 4, THE CINEMA  (a round; entity: the Usher)
Look     An 80s-90s multiplex lost in the backrooms: navy walls, magenta and cyan neon bands, a carpet of pink
         and blue triangles on a dotted grid, a starlight ceiling, an arcade, auditoriums of red seats, a
         projector booth upstairs. THE ROUND STARTS WITH THE POWER OFF: black, with deep red emergency light,
         a CLOSED sign, STAFF ONLY doors. The neon only comes back when you restore the power.
Entity   Very tall and thin, in a crimson bellhop uniform: two rows of gold buttons, gold cuffs, a gold stripe
         down the leg, a crimson pillbox cap with a gold band. Its head is a glossy black mannequin head with
         no face. Its hands are bright white gloves that glow. It only exists in the dark, a torch beam on it
         freezes it, and it whispers "shhh" before it takes you. It sleeps until the power is on.
To clear 1. Read the note: it gives the order for the six breaker switches (A1-A3, B1-B3).
         2. Power comes on. The Usher wakes.
         3. Find three film reels (one can be locked in the arcade's prize case behind a code).
         4. Thread each reel into its projector upstairs. A projector only threads while somebody holds the
            MAIN BREAKER downstairs, or for 60 seconds on a fuse: this is the co-op part.
         5. The film runs and the screen of Cinema 2 is the way out.
This run A real round was started: the red power-off state, all six switches, the note, three reels, the
         prize case, three projectors, the main breaker and the fuse were located. The Usher was photographed
         from the game's own model (it is dormant until the power is on), once in the red light and once under
         a white test lamp to see its true colours. The breaker puzzle was not solved tonight.
NOTE     The older reference showed the Usher as a pale pink glowing figure. Up close it is the red uniform
         and black head described here; the promo pictures follow that.


LEVEL 5, THE VOID  (lives on the lobby server; NO ENTITY)
Look     Ten enormous rooms, each one colour and nothing else: rose, blue, amber, mint, violet, coral, orange,
         crimson, teal, ivory. Smooth matte surfaces, soft light high up, and under the walkways pure black.
         The route is 4130 studs and 171 jumps: narrow ledges and block tops, a double spiral (orange), a
         stair wound round a great pillar (crimson), a field of bare pillar tops (teal), a tower with treads
         missing (ivory). Dark blue balls lie on ledges. Things fall past you in the open rooms: blocks, and
         now and then a body in a yellow suit, screaming.
Entity   None, by the owner's decision. The drop is the monster.
To clear 1. Jump the route. A fall is death and sends you back to the lobby.
         2. Each room ends in a plate: the door only opens while the WHOLE party stands on it.
         3. After the tower, the corridor: 226 studs between two walls that start closing when everyone is in.
            Walk and never stop (walking gets through with 8 studs to spare; standing still dies at 18 s).
         4. LEVEL 5 CLEARED, then on to Level 6 or back to the lobby.
This run A real session: stood in rose, blue, mint, orange, crimson, teal and ivory and at the corridor (moved
         along the route's own points, not jumped tonight). All 171 jumps were walked on 2026-10-05.


LEVEL 6, THE PLAYGROUND  (lives on the lobby server; entity: the Counter)
Look     One colossal indoor soft-play arena, rebuilt on 2026-10-06 and given real materials on 2026-10-07.
         You arrive in a low tunnel padded in yellow and navy quilted vinyl, through a gate that says
         PLAY ZONE. A lane leads to a round court of blue-and-green foam mats with a yellow ring and a short
         red post. All round the court, twelve storeys of netted galleries: vinyl-wrapped posts in four
         colours, black nets, mat decks, red panels with bubble windows, tube slides, and two rope bridges
         crossing high above. Under the court: a slide down to a small, mouldy, green padded room.
Entity   A porcelain ball-jointed doll child, taller than a player: bald cracked white head, large dark glass
         eyes, rosy cheeks, a small smile, a striped paper party hat, a faded striped T-shirt, dusty denim
         dungaree shorts. It counts at the post with its hands over its eyes, then walks toward the nearest
         player, a little faster every round. If it sees you, you are as good as dead (it runs at 40), and
         the kill is a choke seen through your own eyes.
To clear 1. Hide while it counts.
         2. While it searches, every living player has to touch the post. That is one round.
         3. Do it three times. It gets quicker each time.
         4. On the third touch everything turns red and a one-minute countdown starts while it hunts in a rage.
         5. The post sinks. Drop into the hole, ride the long slide, and leave by the green EXIT door.
This run A real session: the tunnel and gate, the court, the Counter at the post from 12 and 5 studs, the view
         down from the eighth floor, the bridges, the exit room. Whole rounds (three searches, the countdown,
         the slide, LEVEL 6 CLEARED) were played earlier today and on 2026-10-06.


WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PICTURES
- Six places that could not be mistaken for each other: yellow office, sunlit pools, party rooms, neon
  cinema, one-colour void, soft-play arena. That is the game's best argument and it leads the thumbnails.
- Four hunters with strong silhouettes: a black hunched thing with two eyes, a man with a balloon for a head,
  a faceless usher with glowing gloves, a porcelain doll in a party hat.
- Levels 2 and 5 have no hunter and are sold on scale: tiny yellow figures in something far too big.
- The promise in the name: teammates hiding, holding still, a finger to the mask.
