## The six levels (the references are the truth; this is what you are looking at)
1. LEVEL 1, THE OFFICE. The classic yellow backrooms: mustard damask wallpaper, beige-brown carpet, low white
   acoustic-tile ceiling with fluorescent panels, square pillars, endless low partitions, black marker scribbles
   on walls. ENTITY, "the Entity": a tall, hunched, pitch-black figure in a rough hooded knit garment, heavy
   shoulders, very long arms, big long-fingered hands, chunky feet, two small round glowing eyes (amber when
   calm, red when hunting). It hears noise. Players pull fuses out of wall cabinets marked ZYNTRA RELAY, carry
   them to fuse boxes, pull levers and run for the exit.
2. LEVEL 2, THE POOLROOMS. BRAND NEW MAP. THERE IS NO ENTITY, NO MONSTER, NO CREATURE AND NO FOAM IN LEVEL 2.
   It is a silent, sunlit, clean, endless bath-house you walk through: a flooded hall of tiled columns and
   vaults over turquoise water; a nave of colossal fluted concrete columns; an immense stepwell of zig-zag
   stairs with glass rails down to a green pool; a domed rotunda in deep blue and gold mosaic with a golden
   gong on an island; a lap-pool hall with ribbon windows onto empty desert; and a ten-storey atrium of
   identical yellow doors over a drained pool. Cream plaster, pale tile, warm daylight. Awe and loneliness.
3. LEVEL 3, THE PARTY ROOMS. A dead mall's children's party rooms: road-map play carpet or dark confetti
   carpet, cream or orange walls with a black lower band, black tile ceiling with a few panels, folding tables
   with paper cups and plates, plastic kids' chairs, balloons, wall stickers. Players collect five CDs in jewel
   cases and hide UNDER the folding tables. ENTITY, "the Mall Manager": very tall and thin; its head is a
   glossy RED BALLOON with no face; short-sleeved shirt with wide orange and cream vertical stripes and a white
   name tag; black trousers, white belt buckle, black shoes; long thin pale arms ending in RED GLOVES. The
   reference shows it in a stiff arms-out rig pose: never draw that pose. Draw it walking, stooping, peering.
4. LEVEL 4, THE CINEMA. An 80s-90s multiplex lost in the backrooms: navy walls, magenta and cyan neon bands, a
   carpet of pink and blue triangles on a dotted grid, a starlight ceiling, arcade cabinets, red seats, a
   glowing screen, a projector booth. With the power off it is black with deep red emergency light. ENTITY,
   "the Usher": very tall and thin, in a crimson bellhop uniform with gold buttons in two rows, gold cuffs and a
   gold stripe down the trouser leg, a crimson pillbox cap with a gold band; its head is a glossy BLACK
   featureless mannequin head with no face; its hands are bright WHITE gloves that glow. It only exists in the
   dark and freezes while a torch beam is on it. Same note: the reference is a stiff rig pose, do not copy the
   pose.
5. LEVEL 5, THE VOID. THERE IS NO ENTITY IN LEVEL 5. Ten huge rooms, each ONE colour (rose, blue, amber, mint,
   violet, coral, orange, crimson, teal, ivory), smooth matte plastic, soft pools of light near the top, and
   below the walkways nothing but black. Players jump along narrow ledges, block tops, pillar tops and spiral
   stairs with treads missing. A fall is death. Dark blue balls lie on some ledges; a small lamp globe hangs on
   a long thin rod here and there.
6. LEVEL 6, THE PLAYGROUND. One colossal indoor soft-play arena: a round court of blue-and-green foam puzzle
   mats with a yellow ring and a short RED padded post in the middle, and all round it twelve storeys of
   netted galleries: posts wrapped in yellow, red, blue and green vinyl, black knotted nets, mat decks, red
   panels with round bubble windows, big tube slides, rope bridges high across the court, cold strip lights,
   loose plastic balls. ENTITY, "the Counter": a porcelain ball-jointed doll child about as tall as a player
   and a half: bald white cracked head, large dark glass eyes, rosy cheeks, a small closed smile, a striped
   paper party cone hat, a striped T-shirt in faded orange, blue and cream, dusty denim dungaree shorts, bare
   jointed limbs. It counts at the post with its hands over its eyes while players hide, then comes looking.
