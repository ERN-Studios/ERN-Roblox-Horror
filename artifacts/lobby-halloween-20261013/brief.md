## Why
The game's lobby is a dead 1990s shopping mall (a Roblox game; see the references). The owner wants it decorated for
Halloween and wants to SEE first how that could look, in his words: "halloween decorated 90s middle class mall style,
with jack o lanterns, and such", plus "halloween sale signs around the lobby and at the shop saying 66,6 halloween
discount on everything" and a re-entry package, three re-entries for the price of one. After his look at your
pictures the decorations are modelled in Blender from simple shapes and placed in the real lobby. So every
decoration you draw must be BUILDABLE from simple low-polygon shapes, and the room itself must stay exactly the room
in the references.

## The look
These are IN-GAME pictures: the game's own simple blocky geometry, flat simple textures, clean hard edges, real-time
game lighting, a wide game camera. NOT a painting, NOT photoreal, no haze, no god rays, no film grain, no lens flare.
The decoration style is a middle-class American mall in October, about 1994: cheap, store-bought, put up by the mall
office and never taken down. Use this kit, and only things like it:
- plastic blow-mould JACK-O'-LANTERNS in three sizes, lit from inside (a warm orange glow, the only new light),
  carved triangle eyes and a toothy grin; on the floor in clusters, on hay bales, on planter edges, on the stage
- HAY BALES, bundles of dried CORN STALKS tied to pillars, pots of orange and yellow mums, a SCARECROW on a pole
- twisted CREPE-PAPER STREAMERS in orange and black, swagged pillar to pillar and along the shop sign boxes
- PENNANT strings (orange/black/purple triangles) across the hall under the ceiling
- die-cut cardboard decorations taped to pillars and walls: jointed SKELETONS, black CATS, BATS, WITCH silhouettes
- BEDSHEET GHOSTS and black rubber BATS hanging from the ceiling on thin lines; fake COBWEB in corners and on the truss
- orange plastic trick-or-treat PAILS, a few orange and black BALLOONS (some sagging on the floor)
- SALE SIGNAGE in the cheap retail style of the time: orange vinyl banners with black block letters, yellow starburst
  price cards, a two-sided floor standee (A-frame), ceiling danglers over the shop stand
The mall is dead and a little wrong (it is the Backrooms): nobody is there, some lamps are out, a streamer has come
loose, one pumpkin sits far out in the dark. Eerie, not gory: NO blood, NO corpses, NO monsters, NO real people.
