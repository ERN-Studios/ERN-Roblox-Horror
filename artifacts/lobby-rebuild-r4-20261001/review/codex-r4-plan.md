## Concrete design requirements

Preserve ServerLobby, center `(0,30,-760)`, and keep rebuilt owned lobby beside it at `(220,30,-760)`. The shared industrial architecture remains a 280-stud long, 35-stud radius semicircular tunnel with 33-stud asphalt roadway, raised sidewalks, 56-stud circular bays, three gate rows, and six distinct destinations. All decorative stacks stay outside open routes and queue cancellation clearances.

R3 still creates all six bays from the same orange/dark floor mesh. Its tunnel arc has 36 segments with flat face normals; its color atlas gives only 128 square pixels per material, projects each polygon independently, and provides no actual normal or roughness maps. These are concrete reasons a rebuild needs real authored surface/UV changes.

Use 96–128 radial subdivisions with shared smooth normals on the inner tunnel surface, mathematically exact common radius and section boundaries, and hard normals on aperture jambs/rims. Avoid smoothing aperture cuts into warped concrete. Keep simple independent colliders and no displacement collision. Do not make all geometry double-sided to hide wrong winding. UV the long wall by circumference versus tunnel length, with repeat scale in studs; keep seams behind ribs or on the floor edge. Root should inspect actual close and long-axis viewpoints before accepting smooth roundness.

Give concrete, asphalt and sidewalk structural families actual color/normal/roughness textures. Concrete needs subdued aggregate/pinholes and formwork seams, sparse stain runs below fixtures and service penetrations, not high-frequency noise evenly covering every panel. Asphalt needs dark neutral aggregate, restrained repair seams, worn lane paint and curb edge grit. Sidewalk joints should follow measured slab dimensions and align across section boundaries. Retain the existing atlas for small props if appropriate. Share reusable materials/maps rather than uploading per placement.

Roblox supports OpenGL tangent-space normal maps and AssetService:CreateSurfaceAppearanceAsync from EditableImage Content. The current baker only applies TextureContent and must acquire a real SurfaceAppearance bake/clone path. Most SurfaceAppearance map properties cannot be written by gameplay scripts. Sequential bounded creation, robust failure cleanup, cached reuse and a directional-light bump check are required. Existing mesh import flips UV V and bitmap rows; preserve its convention and verify actual bump direction instead of blindly adding a second green-channel inversion. Sources: [Roblox PBR guide](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/modeling/surface-appearance), [AssetService API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/AssetService), [SurfaceAppearance API](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/SurfaceAppearance).
