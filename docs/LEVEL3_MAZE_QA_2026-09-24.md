## CD table prompt conflict found and fixed

Two further normal solo Level 3 rounds again reached 2/5 CDs with ordinary movement and E-hold, then ended with the Mall Manager killing the player; the death card correctly said “THE MANAGER FOUND YOU.” In the second round the player reached CD03 in `L3_S2_R03`. At about 7.8 studs, the same E-hold activated `HideUnderTablePrompt` instead of `CollectPrompt`: CD03 stayed `WORLD`, progress stayed 2/5, and the player entered hiding. The Manager reached the player before a second pickup. This was a real prompt conflict, not evidence that the five-CD route is complete.

The root cause was in `Level 3 Hiding Controller`: its existing CD↔table link suppressed HIDE only for the first CD. All five CDs use the first table of their room, which has a hide anchor at the same X/Z. Commit `621c903` links every source CD to exactly one nearby hide anchor, gives COLLECT sole E priority while its state is `WORLD`, and restores HIDE when the CD leaves the table. A live-world Test Suite assertion was added. The changed Hiding Controller and Test Suite were pushed to Studio separately with fresh Source/editor equality and compiled; the full Studio audit returned 182/182.

In a fresh normal Level 3 Studio round before pickup, all **5/5** CD source models were `WORLD` with COLLECT enabled and their colocated HIDE prompts disabled; the other **19/19** hide tables stayed enabled. For a focused interaction probe, a disposable Play-only setup placed the player beside CD03 and raised health to avoid Manager interference. The client camera aimed at the jewel case and used the normal ProximityPrompt E-hold. CD03 changed `WORLD→CARRIED`, server progress became 1/5, and `Level3_Hiding` stayed false. Its HIDE prompt then enabled; a subsequent normal prompt hold entered hiding. This proves the prompt priority and restoration in the engine, **not** natural navigation to CD03 or a full clear. Play was stopped back to Edit. Offline first-CD prompt checks passed 18/18; hidden-chase fixture checks passed 57/57 after repairing its stale protection/table-check setup; all 182 scripts compiled with Luau `-O0`.

## Genuine five-CD rounds and the finale freeze (Claude, 24 September evening)

Full record: [artifacts/level3-solo-20260924/README.md](../artifacts/level3-solo-20260924/README.md). A client bot drove ordinary movement (PathfindingService + `Humanoid:MoveTo` at the game's WalkSpeed) and the engine's prompt holds, starting from lobby station 9 with a normal one-player party. The Mall Manager only exists in 30-second hunts every ~215 s after the first pickup, and hiding under a table makes the player unattackable, so the bot hid ahead of each hunt.

- **Round 2, seed 1428587057**: 5/5 CDs, a real hunt with `TABLE_CHECK` on the bot's table (player stayed at 100 HP), reader 5/5, exit and `Escaped=true`. Every COLLECT prompt showed at 6.2–7.6 studs with the colocated HIDE disabled before and enabled after pickup. **New defect**: the finale Manager froze in L3_S2_R05 and never reached the hall.
- **Root cause** (reproduced deterministically with 10 Hz telemetry; three read-only investigators and two adversarial verifiers agreed): R05 is that seed's only S2→S3 gateway. Its corner columns and two table envelopes leave the Manager's centre one 1.7-stud aisle. Pathfinding routes through an 8.6-stud column/wall gap that the 10.5-stud body sweep rejects, both perimeter-repair rings fail, and every repath returns the same route, so the loop is `PHYSICAL_WAYPOINT_REJECTED` forever.
- **Fix**: `LEVEL3_MANAGER_ROOM_AISLE_REPAIR_20260924` in the Manager AI Controller adds a third fallback to `installRoomPerimeterPath`, pushed by scoped CAS and compiled in Studio. It is a rate-limited one-stud grid search of the current room using the unchanged `volumeFits`, string-pulled with the unchanged `volumeClear`, and it publishes status `ROOM_AISLE`. A first attempt that sized waypoint reach from actual speed only moved the freeze to the doorway and was reverted. After review it was hardened: the doorway leg is mandatory, the search is capped at 2,000 cells, and failed searches back off 1 to 8 s. The hardened source (187,862 B) is in Studio (182/182), but only the pre-hardening version was played. Offline, `tools/tests/test_level3_room_aisle_repair.py` has 29 checks (every guard mutation-checked); steering (222 checks), hidden-chase (57 checks) and `-O0` compile limits (182/182) still pass.
- **Round 7, freeze seed (pre-hardening search)**: the Manager crossed R05 with `ROOM_AISLE` (13.2 ms search) and chased in the hall at a locked 32.8 studs/s.
- **Round 6, random seed 1431515537**: a genuine clear with the working finale chase. The gap closed from 131 to 29 studs, and the runner escaped with 14 % stamina (×1.55 in Studio).
- **Base stamina (×1.0, a disclosed test aid in round 7)**: a perfect sprint escaped, but the Manager entered `ATTACK_WINDUP` three times 3–5 studs behind in the last 50 studs. This is a finale-tuning decision for the owner; nothing was changed.
- **Prompt visibility**: 24/24 COLLECT prompts showed at CD1/CD2 from both long sides at 3.6–4.2 studs (the earlier CD02 "hidden at ~4.8 studs" case) and from the table ends.
- Round 1's finale and round 3's stamina readings are excluded: the console shows `Noclip`, `Third-person` and `Unlimited` toggles from keystrokes this session never sent. A Codex computer-use session was active, and the owner has since stopped it.
