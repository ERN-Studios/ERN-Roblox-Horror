## Seen in Studio (Claude)

Captured with `tools/mobile_qa/ui_audit.py` (the HUD laid out at a device size and photographed in tiles, because
Studio's viewport is a strip of 1250 x 116; `stitch.py` joins them): lobby, shop and Level 1 round HUD at 1366 x 768
(keyboard and mouse) and 1024 x 768 (touch); measured without pictures at 1280 x 720, 1920 x 1080, 1180 x 820 and
820 x 1180. The 3D view behind the HUD repeats per tile and means nothing.

- **Lobby, both:** the HUD is a rail of SHOP / UPGRADES / REWARDS / WHEEL / MUTE / Badges / Help, the token pill and
  the friend-boost card. Nothing on screen says where to go to play. (Review item 1.)
- **Round on a tablet:** the objective is a pill about 190 px wide in the top right corner; collapsed, its title is cut
  ("RESTORE THE POWE") and the count sits on top of it; the two lines of directions are gone. (Review item 2.)
- **Round on a computer:** the objective card has no backing: white title and thin grey 11 px directions straight on the
  picture, hard to read over a lit wall. The item slots show an icon and a key (Q, Z) and no name. Nothing on the HUD
  names the keys for running and sneaking.
- **Shop on a computer:** the window is 575 px of a 1366 px screen (the owner's own half-size preference); the row of
  cards is cut after the third (the fourth shows as "Zy" / "THRE"), body text is about 11 px, the token count is shown
  twice (in the window and in the HUD pill behind it), and two header buttons are icons without a word.
- **Shop on a tablet:** fills most of the screen and reads well; the same fourth card is cut and runs off the screen.
- **Measured faults:** the token pill's "+" is cut at every size; in the shop five texts are cut on a computer (35, +,
  COLORS, DONATE, OWNED) and six on a tablet, with three off the screen. No overlaps and nothing off-screen in the
  lobby or the round HUD at any of the six sizes.
- **Tablets cannot turn to portrait:** `StarterGui.ScreenOrientation` is LandscapeSensor (a place property), so the
  review's portrait findings (T6, T7, item 9) do not apply today.
- **Not looked at:** the party dialog, the death and re-entry screens, spectating, Levels 2 to 6, a real device.
