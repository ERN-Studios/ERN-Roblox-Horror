## Ranked reasons — top five first

1. **Finding and starting the actual game requires understanding a physical queue, with competing lobby destinations.**
   - **Read in code:** the first lobby camera turns toward the DJ stage, not explicitly toward Level 1 (`S/First Entry Guide.LocalScript.lua:24`, `:45`). The four-page tutorial teaches gates, pads and CREATE PARTY only on page three (`:85`, `:98`). The lobby rail foregrounds Shops, Upgrades, Rewards, Wheel and Music (`S/ZyntraStore.LocalScript.lua:164`, `:271`, `:364`); HELP is a small secondary control (`S/First Entry Guide.LocalScript.lua:498`). There is no direct first-round launch button in these reviewed clients.
   - A new host must step onto a pad, interpret **MAXIMUM PLAYERS / WHO CAN JOIN**, and tap CREATE PARTY. The default is six, although a solo player does **not** need to recruit six (`S/RoundUI.LocalScript.lua:657`, `:989`; `V/GameManager.Script.lua:3990`, `:4045`). Someone else's unconfigured pad says HOST CHOOSING PARTY SETTINGS and has no configuration timeout in the station loop (`S/RoundUI.LocalScript.lua:3867`; `V/GameManager.Script.lua:4011`). Walking off the host pad cancels it (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:4005`).
   - **Inferred, medium confidence:** a player who skips the tutorial can wander, shop, or wait for a host instead of entering horror gameplay. Touch adds walking/looking effort and makes the secondary instructions harder to discover. This is the strongest hypothesis for the large pre-round loss, not proof of a mobile-only cause.
   - **Smallest fix:** show one persistent lobby instruction, “Start here: Level 1 → step on a pad → Create Party. You can play alone,” with a marker to its gate; make the existing default-six field explicitly say “up to 6; starts with you.” Add an idle-host timeout.

2. **The onboarding card shrinks the exact instructions and touch targets a phone newcomer needs.**
   - **Read in code, high confidence:** tutorial authoring size is 620×452; `UIScale` uses `min((viewport.X−28)/620, (viewport.Y−28)/452)` (`S/First Entry Guide.LocalScript.lua:122`, `:132`). Body text is 15 px, headers 19 px, NEXT/BACK height 50, touch SKIP height 40 (`:151`, `:161`, `:198`). At a hypothetical 844×390 layout viewport, scale is 0.801: body ≈12 px, NEXT/BACK ≈40 px, SKIP ≈32 px. At 568×320 it is 0.646: body ≈9.7 px, NEXT/BACK ≈32 px, SKIP ≈26 px. These are formula calculations, not device screenshots.
   - Touch-specific wording exists (`:92`), and taps are wired (`:226`, `:232`); the problem is readability/target size, not a missing NEXT button. The HELP opener itself remains **64×30**, with 13 px text (`:504`, `:505`), although the opened HELP panel already avoids touch text shrinkage (`:342`). Tablets with more height fare better.
   - **Inferred, medium-high confidence:** phone users skip or fail to absorb controls/queue instructions. Smaller screens worsen this relative to desktop and larger tablets.
   - **Smallest fix:** apply HELP's existing full-size, scrollable touch layout to the tutorial; keep NEXT/BACK/SKIP and HELP at least 44 px after layout, and put the pad instruction on page one.

3. **On touch, the first task's useful directions disappear after six seconds, sometimes immediately.**
   - **Read in code, high confidence:** objective expansion expires after six seconds (`R/RoundHud.ModuleScript.lua:612`). Desktop renders expanded regardless of that flag; touch hides Guide1/Guide2, count/progress when collapsed (`:901`, `:905`). If the expanded card cannot fit the available height, touch collapses it immediately (`:578`, `:586`); if even the remaining card cannot fit, it is hidden (`:599`, `:945`). A 44 px header hit restores expansion for another six seconds (`:697`, `:625`).
   - The missing lines matter: Level 1 says “Find a fuse relay under the amber lights” and “Pull the fuse out. Carry it to a fuse box”; after pickup it says follow a colored cable (`S/PuzzleUI.LocalScript.lua:28`). Level 1 spoken/subtitled introductory briefing is deliberately disabled (`S/RoundUI.LocalScript.lua:3377`, `:3383`). These recent guidance/beacon improvements exist in the mirror; their publication date is unknown.
   - **Inferred, medium-high confidence:** a touch player learning first-person steering can miss those six seconds and retain only “RESTORE THE POWER.” That is an actual device difference, but affects players who reached a round.
   - **Smallest fix:** keep one next-action sentence visible on touch until the first successful extraction/insertion; make the header explicitly say “Tap for instructions,” and give the initial expansion longer than six seconds.

4. **Several waits separate choosing a pad from receiving a task; slow loading can turn the route into a restart.**
   - **Read in code:** queue fallback is 10 s, shortened to 3 s only when the configured party is full (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:114`, `:4057`). Thus 1/6 can launch after ten seconds; it does not wait for six people. An unconfigured host can keep others waiting indefinitely (finding 1).
   - Published flow teleports to a reserved server (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:3661`, `:3684`). Round entry needs character asset preloading, streamed collidable ground, UI and controls readiness (`S/Round Entry Client.LocalScript.lua:95`, `:109`, `:118`). The shared loading deadline is 60 s (`V/Round Loading Runtime.ModuleScript.lua:5`). The loading presentation has four 1.35 s stages plus 0.35+1.6 s, but entry/start signals can cut it short (`S/RoundUI.LocalScript.lua:1886`, `:3965`, `:4024`); do not add 7.35 s as an unconditional extra wait.
   - Level 1 then has a fallback 19 s elevator ride, with one extra second at t=8: **20 s** before the doors open (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:117`, `:3328`). Tuning can change those fallback values (`R/MasterConfiguration.ModuleScript.lua:63`). RoundStart is recorded after the ride (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:3341`), so “never started” may include people who already queued/teleported.
   - **Inferred, medium confidence:** slower phones/network connections encounter more of the asset/party barrier. Even a ready client waits for the cohort. No latency measurements establish that this happened on the ad day.
   - **Smallest fix:** shorten the Level 1 ride and show the actual next task during it; replace cosmetic loading stages with truthful “waiting for N players”/retry information. Measure queue→teleport→entry→RoundStart separately by device before changing the safety barrier.

5. **Touch must combine first-person turning, finding small world interactions and sustained holds, with little adjustment/help.**
   - **Read in code:** rounds lock the camera to first person with 0.5 min/max zoom (`V/GameManager.Script.lua:1191`). HELP explains “Left stick to move, drag the screen to look” (`S/First Entry Guide.LocalScript.lua:255`), but this appears in optional HELP, not the tutorial's HOW YOU PLAY page. RUN and SNEAK are toggles, allowing a camera finger to be free (`S/NoiseReporter.LocalScript.lua:637`, `:650`); there is no evidence here that touch turning is disabled.
   - Fuse extraction requires a **1.8 s hold**, within 10 studs and with line of sight (`V/Level 1 Systems/PuzzleManager.Script.lua:75`, `:289`, `:918`). The custom prompt does support finger hold/release (`S/Found Footage HUD.LocalScript.lua:186`); precise mouse aiming is not mandatory, but approach, visibility and holding still must be learned.
   - Level 1 illumination steps down as relays are extracted, then has red alert fill (`S/RoundUI.LocalScript.lua:499`). Local relay/lever beacons now help (`S/Level 1 Hardware Client.LocalScript.lua:273`), and the torch has wide spill and close fill (`S/FlashlightController.LocalScript.lua:66`, `:74`). Therefore “no brightness help whatsoever” is false. No player brightness/exposure or camera sensitivity setting was found in the reviewed setting list (`R/ZyntraConfig.ModuleScript.lua:377`) or input code. Roblox's own settings/default camera module are outside this mirror.
   - **Inferred, medium confidence:** mobile screen visibility and unfamiliar drag/hold gestures may make searching/chases harder, especially after directions collapse. Actual turning quality, contrast and prompt size need a device check.
   - **Smallest fix:** add a first-round “drag to look / hold this prompt until full” cue, retain the next-action sentence, and expose a touch look-sensitivity option; validate existing beacon/torch readability before changing lighting.

6. **Queue feedback still uses fixed desktop-width labels, despite a device-aware party dialog.**

   - **Smallest fix:** fit the status label to UIDevice.Safe.Width and safe top inset, and give long denial messages sufficient height.

7. **Rendering/audio work is a credible low-end-device risk, but not a measured retention cause.**
   - **Read in code:** awake lobby arms update poses/shading and BulkMoveTo every render frame, with a broad 750-stud camera cutoff (`S/Lobby Tunnel Reach Client.LocalScript.lua:344`, `:361`, `:415`, `:419`, `:432`). Workspace adoption scans in Found Footage HUD (`:250`) and hum registration (`S/SoundController.LocalScript.lua:243`) are initial/streaming work, not full rescans every frame. 8. **Secondary lobby friction: the attractive tunnel can kill a newcomer, and the DJ panel shrinks.**
