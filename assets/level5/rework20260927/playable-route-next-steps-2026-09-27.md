## What must change, and where

`Level5PreviewAccess` calls `Level 5 Rework Gallery.Build` to make ten **separated** cells with teleport prompts, camera pads and `GalleryOnly=true` (`Level 5 Rework Gallery:239–364`). Its builders use gallery-specific coordinate frames and noncolliding facade details. Copying its entire cells under the playable map would bring teleport pads and overlapping floor/shell geometry into the route. A visual draft must be translated into each gameplay district's local frame, reused as a facade kit where practical, and joined to genuine playable floors, doors, stairs and rails.

The live playable seam is `ServerScriptService.Level 5 Systems.Level 5 Round Adapter.Build` at the `architecture.Build(world, ORIGIN, ...)` call (`Round Adapter:331–365`). The returned manifest is passed to `Level 5 Section Progression.Start` and `Level 5 Window Watcher Encounters.Start`. Leave the adapter's ownership, cleanup, 30,000-descendant assertion, and access flags intact. Replace architecture **inside** its existing builder system:

- `Level 5 Architecture.Build`: shared `K` geometry/material helpers and `house` metadata at lines 419–626; zone A/B authoring at 628–709; calls to the C/D/E and F/G/H modules at 711–712; zone bounds, shell and seven gate frames at 713–795; route/manifests at 890–944. Put `S01` under `A_BalconyAtrium` and `S02` under `B_LowEavesArcade` as named child submodels. The shell/threshold code belongs here; do not create a ninth or tenth `manifest.Zones` entry.
- `Level 5 Neighbourhood Districts.Build`: `C_PastelVillage` around lines 14–70 receives `S03`; `D_FloralTerraces` around 72–120 receives `S04`; `E_DomesticLabyrinth` around 122–158 receives `S10`. ## Instance budget before implementation

The previously measured playable static builder has **27,472 descendants**. The earlier draft ten scenes had about **8,256**; direct overlay would yield roughly **35,728**, above the adapter's **30,000** cap even before runtime objects. The currently installed gallery has **9,937** descendants including its hub, pads, prompts and scene shells; this number is **not** an additive playable estimate because it includes gallery-only objects and some of its ten builders are being revised now. Re-measure each proposed scene after those edits.

The [measured replacement audit](replaceable-geometry-audit.md) lists **8,929 disjoint** descendants in 231 closed upper homes and F/G ornamental balcony siblings that can be replaced in the same step as their new facades. It also lists **4,343 conditional** descendants in playable/route-adjacent homes, not safely reclaimable until their floors, clues, Watcher supports and paths are rebuilt. Using the old 8,256 draft only, the first group would yield ~26,799 static descendants, leaving just 201 below the desired 27,000 working target. Reserve room for gameplay and visual corrections; recount actual full world after each district, and never increase the adapter's 30,000 assertion to hide an overrun. Upper trim unions already included in the baseline must not be counted again.

## Phased build and acceptance

1. **Freeze a fresh baseline per edit.** Check `git status`, current remote history, preserve a recoverable checkpoint, read target live Source **and editor** and exact neighboring instances/assets. Prepare one scoped district patch. Inside the Studio write, compare the fresh Source/editor baseline again; if changed, reread and reconcile. Export exact live source/property/asset hashes after verification and retain the native place backup.
2. **Complete camera-matched reference drafts.** For each of 01–10, capture a portrait camera and compare an environment-only crop against the supplied still, including facade bay count, gables, windows, rail pitch, ceiling grid, luminance and perspective. The original attachments are not files in this checkout, so a defensible pixel overlay currently needs those actual image files; until then record a qualitative comparison and do not call any view 1:1.
3. **Replace A/B, then C/D/E.** Keep old collision and clue homes until the new model passes an avatar walk. Retire closed upper models only alongside equivalent new silhouette. At each district, check local view, gate approach, clue home selection and actual Watcher anchor; document descendant delta. Where the existing zone shell or bounds block the view, revise them with the district in the same isolated test.
4. **Replace F and G last.** F requires landing, ledge, staircase, bridge, pit recovery, clue and gate-6 tests from the running server. G needs independent #05/#06/#09 cameras, a walkable change of elevation, a route around the black architectural void, the arrow clue and a turn to gate 7. H is a regression boundary, not a place to merge the ten scenes.
5. **Run full Play checks before publication.** Test owner DEV gallery entry, all ten cells and return; a non-DEV client cannot enter; playable spawn and all seven gates/puzzles in order; all eight Watcher districts; F fall recovery and no unintended shortcut; G→H descent; reset/cleanup; client streaming and collision at distant zone coordinates; server descendant count ≤30,000 plus bounded build time/memory. Capture camera evidence from the *playable* world, not just the separate gallery. Only then commit inspected scoped diffs, save, publish the current Studio place to the existing experience, and verify Roblox's success response. If visual or runtime checks still fail materially, record the blocker and do not publish.

The practical answer to “why does Play still look unchanged?” is that normal Play enters `Round Adapter.Build` → `Architecture.Build`; the current new designs live only behind the developer gallery door. The next meaningful visible milestone is a verified A/B playable replacement in that pipeline, followed by the remaining districts.
