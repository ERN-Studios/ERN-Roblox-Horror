# Reference 05 correction plan — sloped houses between rounded towers

Evidence: the supplied portrait screenshot, the isolated [draft render](draft-ref05.jpg), and `buildSection5` in `Level 5 Reference Anomalies.ModuleScript.lua` (read 2026-09-27). The current section-5 code is still the 708-descendant draft rendered in the QA image. This is a correction plan, not an implemented or 1:1 result. Its Studio render used a simplified test kit and different lighting, so judge shape before colour.

## Change order

1. **Match the camera before spending parts.** The current camera is `(0, 6, 8)` looking at `(6, 30, 150)` in a wide 1920×1076 capture. At that position the front ends of the towers at x≈±50, z≈45 fall near or outside the horizontal edges, so the screenshot mainly shows long flat inner walls. Test a lower portrait crop with the camera farther back (initial search around z=-20 to -35, eye y≈4–6), looking higher toward the house group. Leave the visible lawn in the lower frame and both near rounded tower ends in the upper frame. Record camera/FOV after aligning those landmarks; moving the camera alone will not fix the other shapes.
2. **Rebuild the tower silhouette and camera-facing facade.** `TowerMass` is one rectangular block per side. `RoundedBalconyCorner` and `RoundedFrontCorner` round only isolated slab edges while `FrontWrapBalcony` and its top rail remain straight across the near end. The reference has two prominent rounded or polygonally curved tower ends, stacked wraparound balcony profiles, dense pale vertical rails, black gridded windows and tall arched openings. Segment the visible near tower footprint, slabs and rail path around each projecting end; preserve distinct side and front window bays. Use the existing window and arch code as a starting point, then compare bay count, arch size and balcony projection at the corrected camera. Avoid adding decorative curves behind a still rectangular foreground face.
3. **Move and redesign the house cluster.** Five small `gabledHouse` instances are spread from `s=18` to `98` down the 120-stud incline. They read as isolated, mostly upright one-storey cottages on a broad grass ramp. The reference concentrates larger, visibly askew, multi-window house fronts near the high part of the incline. Shift the visible group upslope and make 3–4 differentiated house masses with actual clapboard courses, white-trimmed divided windows, gables, porch/landing rails and distinct roof pitches. The current transform keeps only `lean × 28°` of pitch (roughly 6–21°) plus 5–13° roll; tune pitch, roll, overlap and yaw in the matched portrait camera so the facades visibly lean without losing readable doors/windows. Do not repeat one generic facade.
4. **Expose the structural underside.** The current 8-stud `PaleStructuralUnderside`, three ribs and high-end wall lie largely hidden directly below the green slab; the render shows only a narrow pale strip. The reference makes a substantial pale diagonal support visible beneath and beside the dark green slope. Shape an offset/tapered side face and soffit with a readable projecting lower edge, and keep the under-slope void/entry relationship visible. Check collision against the lawn and any passage before treating that form as playable.
5. **Reveal real external stairs.** Ten switchback flights exist in code, but their z≈42 plane sits behind/inside near-front balcony slabs at z≈40–49 and front rails at z≈39.8. They are barely legible in the reference-camera render. Reposition the flights and landings to a visible tower edge, expose their alternating direction, add visible treads or riser rhythm, and connect rails/landings without sealing openings. Preserve the current `Walkable`, `DropProtection` and `DropGuard` intents, then test avatar traversal separately. The current upper route is not wired into gameplay.
6. **Tune the enclosed court after geometry.** The screenshot is a dim, warm-lit enclosed grass court under a tile ceiling with rectangular panels. The isolated render looks cool and bright; because it did not reproduce round Lighting or all material variants, verify colour, haze and fixture warmth in the actual Level 5 context. A new surface texture, if needed, must be generated with ChatGPT image gen per the owner's instruction; the existing generated clapboard candidate has not been applied.

## Rough instance impact

The current builder measured **708 descendants** in Studio. Camera work and relocation of existing parts cost approximately zero. Replacing the rectangular tower frontage with visible curved profiles/rails may add roughly **50–90** instances; fuller distinct house facades **40–80** net; exposed support **5–15**; legible stair treads/rails **40–80** net. Working estimate: **+135–265**, or about **845–975 descendants** for this isolated section. These are planning ranges, not measured counts. Recount in Studio after each geometry pass and remove superseded slabs, rails and houses rather than stacking replacements. The full Level 5 has a 30,000-descendant preview cap, so this section must replace old scene geometry during integration.

Acceptance requires a matched portrait render against reference 05 plus side-angle checks for facade openings, incline clearance, stairs, collisions and reachable landings. No visual or gameplay check currently passes that standard.
