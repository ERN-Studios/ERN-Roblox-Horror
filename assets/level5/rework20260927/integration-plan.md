## Gameplay contracts to preserve

- **Zone/gate identity:** `manifest.Zones` must still have eight actual models A–H in order; Progression and Outage assert eight (`Section Progression:92-111`; `Power Outage:40-58`). Seven puzzle gates use the fixed `SectionGates` manifest and open serially; gate 7 leads to `H_LastHouse` (`Section Progression:175-208, 272-303`; `Puzzle Catalog:9-16, 60-76`). Visual submodels are children, not extra `Zones` entries or gates.
- **Clues:** Gate 1 requires exactly one unique numbered `PuzzleClueIndex` 1–4 and a `PuzzleClueSurface` in each of four A homes (`Architecture:640-645`; `Section Progression:354-381`). Gates 2–7 select enterable `HousePuzzleCandidate` homes with a `HouseFloorFrame` and `PuzzleHintSurface` from their *preceding* zone; gate 3 needs three such C homes, the other later gates one or more (`Section Progression:305-353`). F also explicitly asserts an accessible ground television clue house (`Landmark Districts:149-166`). If a replacement moves a clue, preserve the actual walkable approach and recheck selection order by Z/X.
- **Watcher:** Keep at least one supported tinted glass anchor in each A–H zone, including H's existing quiet neighbour; the Window Watcher validates a genuine glass pane, floor pose, support raycasts and district ownership (`Architecture:600-616, 920-930`; `Window Watcher Encounters:167-209`). Decorative dark panes cannot substitute for these. Preserve `Level5TintedWindow`, `HouseRoomSize`, `ActualFloorTopY`, pane dimensions and correct lower/upper backing floor at every anchor.
- **F traversal:** The main F route is a sequence of ledges, four main flights, crossings and an escape from the pit; `Architecture` replaces its broad default waypoints with `L.FRouteWaypoints` (`Landmark Districts:74-112, 168-215`; `Architecture:892-905`). Update the route and rescue/clue waypoints atomically with any F floor change. Do not use a scenic stair as the only player path.
- **G/H transition and final area:** G's gate-7 arrow clue must remain reachable before the threshold. H retains `FinalHouse`, `NarrowDescent`, its route/chute endpoint and 48 ceiling eye pairs (`Landmark Districts:315-469`; `Architecture:791-879, 940-943`). Outage reads H bounds and chute geometry from the actual zone model and requires ceiling fixtures in every zone (`Power Outage:83-124`).
- **Preview/runtime:** Both DEV static preview and playable round call the same `Architecture.Build`. They each enforce a 30,000 descendant cap (`Level5PreviewAccess:193-195`; `Round Adapter:331-365`). Scene builders must replace old geometry inside the eight zone models; attaching all drafts to the old map breaks this cap and leaves the old facades in view.

## Replacement budget and integration order

The measured current static build is **27,472 descendants** after the F trim unions. The ten isolated draft builders total **3,491** descendants; attaching them all produces roughly **30,963**, before return prompt or round-owned objects (`qa-baseline.md`, `groundwork-review.md`). Those drafts are schematic and their counts are **not** final-quality budgets. Adopt a working static target **at or below 27,000**, leaving about 3,000 for runtime additions and correction. With only the current drafts, this requires removing **at least 3,963** old descendants; every added final facade detail needs corresponding old geometry removed or consolidated.
