The current grass reads desaturated teal, tower plaster nearly white, and the dark ceiling navy blue. Try warmer, lower-value *section-local* colors on tower mass, slab undersides, rear void, berm, and lawn texture tint; keep rails and rectangular ceiling fixtures bright. Preserve some warm scattered window light and deepen the recess behind it. Check the existing candidate lawn texture `rbxassetid://108216315862080` at 24 studs/tile in `Level 5 Rework Gallery` lines 139–152 before requesting new texture work. Do not change global `Lighting` based on this one gallery screenshot; first capture the same camera in Play/round conditions to separate material choices from environment exposure.
3. **Make all three bridges visibly tiered** (lines 639–660; live 586–607). Keep three distinct z/y pairs, but inspect them in a matched portrait view before adjusting placement. The current top bridge is visible; the far two collapse into faint horizontal strokes. Increase the visible soffit/side depth and rail-post rhythm where they recede, while retaining `Walkable`, `DropProtection`, and invisible `DropGuard` roles. Do not imply they connect to the playable route; they are currently `Level5Reachability="Unwired"`.
4. **Give the far houses distinct fronts** (lines 677–685 and `gabledHouse` lines 175–208; live 624–632). The four pale roofs currently form an almost identical row of tiny icons. Stagger depth and widths, vary roof pitch/color and window/door grouping, add a small porch or dark window trim to the two most legible houses, and keep the cluster tiny against the towers. The reference cottages have light walls but are readable as individual houses, not triangular markers. Avoid a generic cloned facade; only one currently receives the helper's divided window detail (`detail=i == 2`).
5. **Lock the image composition before judging materials** (lines 688–690; live 635–637). The source is portrait, while current captures are 1677×1080 landscape at the viewport FOV. Capture an environment-only 9:16 frame or an equivalent measured center crop, then tune camera position/aim/FOV against the reference's near ceiling, bridge tiers, tiny distant houses, and large uninterrupted dark lawn. A landscape capture or a new FOV alone cannot prove a 1:1 match. Check that no ceiling/side gap reveals sky.
