Generated on 2026-08-27 as darker, more frightening lighting edits of the Studio-accurate v3 carousel set. The live Roblox Studio place (`placeId: 131311258779917`) remains the source of truth for the avatar, entities, maps, and objectives.

The PNG files are the edited ImageGen source renders (1672 × 941). The JPEG files are upload-ready 1920 × 1080 exports at quality 92.

## Accuracy retained from v3

- Default player: the exact `StarterPlayer.StarterCharacter` mustard/olive suit, featureless black visor, black gloves and boots, and ribbed black backpack.
- Level 1 Entity: the exact giant corrupted hazmat silhouette, oversized boots, floor-length arms, long black claws, and tiny amber-yellow visor pinpoints.
- Pool Foam: the exact tall knot of glossy primary-color foam tubes with no face.
- Slidemouth: the exact low four-legged playground-pipe monster, yellow-rimmed circular mouth, cream teeth, and no eyes or separate head.
- Mall Manager: the exact tall thin humanoid with a smooth red head, faded orange shirt, long pale arms, red hands, and black trousers.
- Map layouts, props, proportions, camera positions, and character poses remain those of the approved v3 compositions.

## Horror treatment

- Broad ambient illumination was reduced substantially rather than changing the creature designs.
- Failing fluorescents, cold cyan water light, flashlight isolation, CRT static, haze, deeper shadows, vignette, and restrained VHS grain increase tension while preserving thumbnail readability.
- The brighter v3 originals remain untouched for comparison and fallback.
