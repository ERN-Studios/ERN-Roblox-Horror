# Dark horror edit prompt record

All five images were edited individually from the approved Studio-accurate v3 PNGs using the built-in ImageGen image-editing workflow. The edits were restricted to lighting, atmosphere, and color grading: exact subjects, entity anatomy, avatar design, map geometry, props, camera composition, and poses were to remain unchanged. No title, logo, captions, HUD, watermark, border, gore, or new creature features were requested.

## 01 — Level 1 corner chase

Edit lighting and weather only. Turn the scene into a partial power failure and reduce broad ambient illumination by roughly 45 percent. Keep only the fluorescent panel immediately above the Entity alive, casting a narrow sickly green-yellow cone; let the side corridors fall into deep black. Add a faint cold rim on the avatar, suspended dust and haze, stronger vignette, subtle VHS grain, and deeper contact shadows. Preserve the exact avatar, Entity, amber-yellow visor pinpoints, map, pose, framing, and all geometry.

## 02 — Level 1 flashlight ambush

Make the maze almost completely dark. The player's flashlight becomes the dominant light source, catching the patterned wall and the Entity's enormous long black fingers. Retain only the two tiny amber visor pinpoints and one distant weak flickering ceiling panel. Add floating dust, light haze, heavy edge darkness, restrained VHS grain, and oppressive contrast. Preserve the exact subjects, anatomy, camera, map, pose, and composition.

## 03 — Level 2 Pool Foam kids room

Create a blackout/night version of the exact pool scene. Use dim cold midnight-blue skylight and let two cyan underwater pads provide the strongest illumination. Rim-light the real Pool Foam while preserving its exact glossy colored tube design and keeping it completely faceless. Give the avatar a cold edge light; push the arches into black, add low water mist, dark reflections, vignette, and subtle VHS texture. Preserve every model, prop, pose, camera angle, and map feature.

## 04 — Level 2 Slidemouth Grand Slide Hall

Turn the exact hall into a near-total facility blackout. Add dim stormy blue-gray shafts, cyan submerged path lights, and a cold rim on the avatar. Give the teeth and yellow mouth rim sharp sparse highlights while keeping the mouth an absolute black abyss; leave the real red, green, blue, and yellow pipe body and four legs readable mainly through wet reflections. Darken columns and slides, add fog, vignette, and subtle VHS grain. Preserve exact anatomy and add no eyes, separate head, flesh, or extra limbs.

## 05 — Level 3 Mall Manager disc relay

Create a post-CD power failure in the exact party room. Switch off most ceiling panels and leave one violently flickering cold panel. Let the real CRT/VCR cast a faint sickly green static glow with tiny status lights. The Mall Manager should emerge from red-black shadow with only thin cold highlights defining its exact smooth red head, faded orange shirt, pale arms, red hands, and black trousers. Add a cold edge on the avatar, haze, long shadows, stronger vignette, and VHS grain. Preserve exact models, TV/VCR unit, room, pose, framing, and composition.
