# Backrooms: No Way Out — Roblox experience description

> Live public copy accepted by Roblox moderation on 2026-08-27.

You have been assigned to TEAM ALPHA.

Enter unstable anomalous spaces, complete each mission, and reach the ZYNTRA exit. Every level has a different objective, threat, and way out. The deeper you go, the harder it becomes for Command Center to reach you.

EXPLORE shifting liminal spaces  
SOLVE cooperative objectives  
EVADE mysterious inhabitants  
FOLLOW Command Center briefings and your energy reader  
ESCAPE alone or with up to 6 players

LEVELS 1-3 AVAILABLE

Level 1: Restore power and synchronize the levers.  
Level 2: Activate the pumps and reach the exit slide.  
Level 3: Recover five CDs and escape the mall.

One life per round. Stay together. Keep moving.  
Headphones recommended.

EARLY ACCESS - More anomalous spaces are under investigation.
