# Roblox Ads v1 — Stay Quiet creative test

Created on 2026-09-01 for **BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET [CO-OP HORROR]**.

## Campaign design

This package uses 15 Home-feed creatives: five creative hypotheses with three images each. Fifteen is large enough to test meaningfully without splitting delivery across 25 near-duplicate images, and it leaves ten campaign slots available for later iterations of the winning concepts.

1. **Silence mechanic** — can decoy, broken glass, static CRT.
2. **Close encounters** — hiding under a desk, a hand around the doorway, a full chase.
3. **Iconic worlds** — party room, flooded Pool Foam chamber, Slidemouth pipe hall.
4. **Co-op tension** — shush signal, coordinated decoy, closing gate.
5. **Escape and mystery** — elevator reflection, security-feed reveal, wrong door.

## Deliverables

- `01`–`15` PNG files are the built-in ImageGen source renders.
- Matching `01`–`15` JPEG files are upload-ready 1920 × 1080 exports at quality 92 and below 3 MB.
- `campaign-contact-sheet.jpg` is a numbered overview for review only; do not upload it as a sixteenth creative.
- `campaign-manifest.json` records ordering, test hypothesis, hook, and pending Ads Manager settings.
- `generation-prompts.md` records the shared visual brief and every scene delta.
- The approved square Search/default thumbnail is `../../banners/content-stay-quiet.png`; it is separate from the 15 Home creatives.

## Creative rules

- One instantly readable threat, action, or decision per frame.
- Consistent blocky tan/yellow hazmat player with ribbed black backpack.
- Cinematic Roblox gameplay presentation, not live action.
- Strong practical lighting and clear silhouettes at small feed size.
- Creature-based, bloodless horror with no gore.
- No title text, fake UI, unrelated logos, or watermarks.
