# Backrooms: Stay Quiet — Roblox thumbnail carousel v6

Five upload-ready 16:9 carousel thumbnails, ordered for the Roblox experience page:

1. `01-level1-fuse-relay-ambush.jpg` — objective interaction plus immediate threat
2. `02-level1-corner-chase.jpg` — clean chase/action read
3. `03-level1-hold-the-elevator.jpg` — co-op rescue story
4. `04-level2-pool-foam-second-pump.jpg` — saturated Level 2 variety
5. `05-level3-mall-manager-blackout.jpg` — red/teal Level 3 blackout

## Delivery specs

- Final format: JPEG
- Final dimensions: 1920 × 1080
- Source masters: `source-scenes/*.png` at 1672 × 941
- Brand font: DIN Condensed Bold
- Brand colors: warning yellow `#F6C744`, white, and translucent black
- Text is deterministic and remains readable around a 320 × 180 card size.

The raw no-text scene masters are kept so the title treatment can be revised without regenerating the artwork. The built-in ImageGen workflow and the final prompt set are documented in `generation-prompts.md`.

## Accuracy decisions

- Level 1 uses the current giant corrupted hazmat Entity and canonical mustard/olive player suit. Because all three scenes depict player detection, its exactly two visor lights use the red hunt state rather than the amber idle state.
- Level 2 uses the currently present Pool Foam entity and current saturated slide-hall palette.
- Level 3 uses the current red-headed Mall Manager in its faded orange uniform.
- Retired Slidemouth artwork was not reused.
- The large Pool Slide tube-humanoid was not advertised because its template/controller/client were absent from the connected Studio place during the final accuracy audit.

No Roblox upload was performed as part of this asset pass.
