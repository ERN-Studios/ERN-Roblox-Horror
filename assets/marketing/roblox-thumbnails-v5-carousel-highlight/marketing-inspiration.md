# Backrooms marketing inspiration notes

Reviewed on 2026-08-27. These are presentation patterns, not visual assets to copy.

## Visual patterns

- Successful Backrooms imagery makes the player silhouette and one threat readable immediately.
- Bright hazmat suits, flashlights, fluorescent pools of light, or colored objective paths create strong focal contrast against repetitive dark environments.
- Chase direction and scale differences communicate the game loop faster than wide empty screenshots.
- Liminal architecture remains visible enough to identify the level, but corners and exits stay dark to preserve mystery.

## Description patterns

- Lead with the simple goal: explore, solve, and escape.
- Name solo/co-op support early.
- Use short feature bullets instead of a large paragraph.
- End with a direct survival question to create suspense.
- Keep warnings and platform information separated from the main hook.
