# Roblox experience thumbnails — gameplay-accurate hazmat set

Five replacement images for the **Backrooms: No Way Out** experience-detail carousel. This version replaces the earlier black tactical avatars with the shared in-round hazmat gameplay rig and gives every image a level-specific objective, environment, and threat.

## Intended order

1. `01-level1-fuse-relay-hazmat.jpg` — Level 1 fuse extraction beneath the bright ceiling-light clue while the Entity approaches.
2. `02-level1-lever-sync-hazmat.jpg` — Level 1 colored cables and synchronized lever activation during the Entity chase.
3. `03-level2-pump-poolfoam-hazmat.jpg` — Level 2 pump activation in shallow water beside the children's play area and Pool Foam entities.
4. `04-level2-exit-tube-slidemouth-hazmat.jpg` — Level 2 upper-deck exit-tube chase with the referenced Slidemouth design.
5. `05-level3-cd-relay-mall-manager-hazmat.jpg` — Level 3 five-CD TV/VCR relay, hidden PA corridor, and referenced Mall Manager.

## Character continuity

Every player uses the same recognizably blocky Roblox hazmat design: mustard-yellow hooded coverall, black full-face respirator with two round lenses, black gloves and boots, compact dark equipment pack, and small cyan ZYNTRA accents. No exposed face or hair and no black tactical uniform.

## Files

- The JPEG files are upload-ready at 1920×1080 and remain below Roblox's 3 MB limit.
- The matching PNG files preserve the original ImageGen output at 1672×941.
- All compositions are text-free and avoid UI, logos, watermarks, and gore.

## Generation references

The built-in ImageGen workflow used the definitive Level 1 full-body silhouette and buried screaming-face references, the Slidemouth turnaround, the Mall Manager front reference, the Level 2 pump material, and the approved first hazmat render as the continuity anchor for the remaining four images. The corrected Level 1 Entity has no glowing red eyes: it keeps the dense hand-scribbled strand body, extremely long arms and claws, and faint human scream hidden inside its head.

## Publication state

This replacement set is prepared and committed separately from the currently published first campaign. It has not replaced the live Roblox carousel yet.
