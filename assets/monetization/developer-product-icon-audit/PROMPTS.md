# Built-in imagegen prompts for the three replacement icons

Mode: built-in `image_gen`, one `stylized-concept` generation per product.
All inputs were reference images, not edit targets. The generated source PNGs
were copied into `proposed/` before deterministic 512 px normalization.

## Expedition Pack

Reference: `assets/shop/box-expedition-pack.png`.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Roblox Developer Product icon for the Expedition Pack in BACKROOMS: STAY QUIET. The attached image is a reference for the exact product contents and industrial black/gold/teal material language, NOT an edit target. Generate a fresh polished square 3D game-store icon. Center one compact armored expedition supply case with a gold-black shell, a bold cyan shield emblem on its front, a distinct small red circular return arrow on one side, and exactly three cyan route marker canisters neatly at the base. All critical shapes must remain fully inside a centered circle covering about 80% of the square, with dark vignette in the corners, so Roblox's automatic round crop cannot cut them. At 128px circular display, case and shield should read immediately. No words, numbers, price, currency symbol, logo or watermark. Rich high contrast lighting, Zyntra teal and red accents, physically rendered materials.
