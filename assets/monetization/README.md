## Shared art direction

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset type: Roblox game monetization icon
- Style: polished semi-realistic 3D industrial sci-fi inventory icon
- Background: Zyntra research-terminal vignette, `#070B0D` / `#141D21`
- Accent: Zyntra teal `#44DDC4`
- Token accent: gold `#FFCB4F`
- Emergency accent: red `#F45F52`
- Composition: central subject with generous safe margin for circular crop
- Constraints: no words, numbers, Robux symbols, logos, watermarks, weapons, or edge-critical details; readable at 64 x 64

## Final prompt set

### Zyntra Supporter

Centered chest-up yellow hazmat operator with a clean teal illuminated supporter badge and three small golden research chips, framed by a dark circular industrial research-terminal vignette. Premium grounded 3D rendering, teal rim light, gold accents, strong central silhouette.

### Advanced Equipment

Centered advanced hazmat helmet and respirator module with a thick color-spectrum calibration arc, one teal stamina upgrade chevron and one gold battery upgrade chevron. Dark industrial research-terminal framing and high-contrast 3D rendering.

### Glowstick Customizer

Exactly three thick rugged emergency glowsticks arranged in a triangular fan: cyan, magenta, and warm yellow, with a simple luminous color-selection ring behind them. Dark industrial circular frame, no flashlight or hazmat helmet.

### 4 Research Tokens

Exactly four clearly countable chunky square golden research microchips, each with a teal core, arranged as a compact diamond cluster on a dark industrial research platform.

### 20 Research Tokens

A large dense cache of many chunky square golden research microchips with teal cores, stacked in three substantial layers with a brighter master chip on top. It must look clearly more valuable than the four-token pack.

### Emergency Re-entry

A sealed industrial elevator/airlock door glowing emergency red with one thick luminous circular return arrow wrapping around the door and pointing inward. Small teal status lights, urgent high contrast, no person or gore.

The complete generation prompts also specified square framing, central safe-area composition, Zyntra palette, 64 x 64 legibility, and all shared constraints listed above.
