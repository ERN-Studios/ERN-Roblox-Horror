### Security and money

1. **ZyntraAction spam no longer costs DataStore writes.** Refusals happen in
   memory first; a transform that returns false cancels `UpdateAsync`; upgrades
   and colour saves have a 1 s per-action window (0.12 s for the rest);
   `SetMuteDispatch` sits behind an escalating 0.75 → 2 → 6 s floor.
2. **A gamepass grant cannot be lost for the session.** `ownsPass` retries and
   answers "unknown" rather than false; a finished purchase latches for the
   session; a failed read is re-asked in the background (3 × 20 s).
3. **Emergency Re-entry re-checks the round after every yield** and returns
   false (so `useReentry` refunds) if the round ended underneath it.
4. **Level 1 prompts are revalidated server-side** (`canUsePrompt`, shaped like
   Level 2's `canUsePump`): living, participating, not escaped, in range, line
   of sight for the relay.
5. **Pool Foam proximity latch** (`Observation.ProximityLatchRadius` 8,
   `ProximityLatchSeconds` 7, `ProximityLatchMaximumSpeed` 3): a client that
   never reports a look is latched when it stands still on the foam. The review
   round found the first numbers (26 studs, 2.5 s) made it the primary latch;
   the shipped ones only catch a camper or a spoofer.

## Open decisions and follow-ups

- Proximity latch numbers (8 / 7 s / 3 studs/s) are the conservative end; a
  spoofer who keeps moving is still never latched. Stronger variants are in
  `docs/AUDIT-2026-09-04.md` and the reviewer's notes in the workflow journal.
- The SETTINGS tab is lobby-only because the terminal opener stands down in
  rounds; a photosensitive player who meets the blackout for the first time has
  to leave the round to reach it. Product decision.
- `BLACKOUT_FLICKER_INTERVAL` 0.17 is a feel change; 0.16 is the fastest value
  still under 3 flashes/s.
- JumpscareUI's kill cam and Pool Foam's FieldOfView punch still ignore
  `ReduceCameraShake`; `CaptionsEnabled` only has a reader in Level 2.
- Pump cue loudness (0.45 at the lever, 60-stud plateau) wants a two-client
  ear test; raise the rolloff minimum before the volume.
- `Level 2 Round Adapter.ModuleScript.lua:393` still names a Pool Slide archive
  folder that does not exist.
- Trello holds the rest: Pool Foam audio, robustness (two stranding cases),
  controller bindings, Emergency Re-entry window length.
