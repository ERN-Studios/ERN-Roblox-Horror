# Level 6 in PBR

Owner, 2026-10-06: "recreate level 6 with PBR textures for every texture where it makes sense, and the floor ...
almost everything would look much better with a detailed, extensive PBR look." Marker: `PBR_20261006`.

Until now nothing in the arena had a normal or a roughness map. It was about 11 000 flat SmoothPlastic and
Concrete parts carrying 16 000 see-through overlay pictures for seams and dirt, so no lamp ever left a highlight
on anything. The look to hit is the approved concept (`artifacts/level6-concept-20261006b`): interlocking foam
mats in a blue and green check with a worn sheen, fat glossy stitched vinyl on every wall, post and panel, scuffed
moulded slides.

## The sets

`make_pbr.py` makes seven sets, each a colour, a normal and a roughness map of 1024 x 1024 that repeats without a
seam, into `assets/level6-pbr-20261006/`. Nothing is photographed or generated by a model: it is all arithmetic
(noise shaped in the frequency domain, which is what makes it repeat).

| Set | MaterialVariant | Repeat | Worn by |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `foam_floor` | L6 Foam Floor | 8 studs | the hall floor, the court, the lid, every deck of the frame |
| `vinyl_quilt` | L6 Vinyl Quilt | 7 | panels, soft blocks, tunnel, gate, rim, shaft, and a pad under every deck |
| `vinyl_plain` | L6 Vinyl Plain | 6 | posts, beams, steps, punch bags, bridge rails |
| `vinyl_filthy` | L6 Vinyl Filthy | 7 | the exit room's pads |
| `block_wall` | L6 Block Wall | 16 | the hall's walls |
| `roof_deck` | L6 Roof Deck | 16 | the roof |
| `slide_plastic` | L6 Slide Plastic | 8 | slides (over their vertex colours), tube windows, balls |

Plus `net_knotted.png`, which is not a set: a material cannot be see-through, so the nets keep their Textures and
only get a better picture (twisted cord, knots).

`preview_pbr.py` renders a corner of the arena wearing them in Blender (`artifacts/level6-pbr-20261006/preview`,
and a `.blend`), which is how they were judged before anything was uploaded.

## Getting the pictures into Roblox

They must belong to the group that owns the experience. Three ways were tried on 2026-10-06:

- `AssetService:CreateAssetAsync` from the session: "not available yet". It is a Studio Beta Feature
  (`CreateAssetAsyncBetaFeature` in the binary; File > Beta Features) and it is off on this Mac
  (`defaults read com.roblox.RobloxStudio BetaFeatureInformation`: `CreateAssetAsync = { enabled = 0 }`,
  `enrolled = 0`). Enrolling is the owner's to accept. That is why it "comes and goes".
- The MCP `upload_image` tool: uploads to the logged-in user. The experience may then not load the picture, not
  even in a Studio play session ("The experience doesn't have access permission to use asset id ...", fetch
  status Failure), until "Share access" is clicked for that one asset in the Output window.
- **Asset Manager > Import**: all files at once, owned by the group. This is the one used.

      python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/collect_ids.py --folder     # ~/Desktop/Level 6 PBR - import these
      (the owner imports that folder's 22 files in Studio's Asset Manager)
      python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/collect_ids.py              # names -> ids, into pbr_ids.json

File names carry the round (`L6PBR_r1_...`); raise `ROUND` in `collect_ids.py` when a picture changes.

## Dressing the level

    python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/apply_pbr.py --dry     # count
    python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/apply_pbr.py --check   # in a play session: can every picture be loaded?
    python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/apply_pbr.py --play    # dress a running play session only, to look
    python3 tools/level6_playground/pbr/apply_pbr.py           # dress the saved place

What it does is in that file's header. The points that are not obvious:

- **A deck is floor above and ceiling below, and a material is the same on every face.** So the deck itself
  becomes the foam mat and gets a thin pad on its underside (`Frame_DeckPads`, 3152 parts that physics and rays
  ignore) in the deck's old colour.
- **The mat's check is in the colour map**, so floor parts turn white. If the pictures cannot be loaded the floors
  would be white plastic: never apply to the saved place before `--check` passes, and do not publish in between.
- **About 10 300 overlay Textures go** (the flat seam pictures the materials replace); the model ends up with
  fewer instances than before although it gains the pads.
- A MaterialVariant lies correctly on blocks, on cylinders and on meshes without UVs (tested with an old picture).
  A MaterialVariant does NOT draw from an EditableImage (`ColorMapContent = Content.fromObject(...)` shows the
  plain base material), so there is no way to look at a set in the engine before it is a real asset.
- Five slides are baked per server by the game module; it gives them `L6 Slide Plastic` itself.
- It runs again after every `import_arena.py`.
